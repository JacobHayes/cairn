# Which decisions a condition reads as unanswered

- Question: Gating makes a decision that is "skipped, `not_relevant`, or itself undecided" unanswered and lets only an open, relevant decision contribute `undecided`. It does not say whether a decided decision whose relevance is undecided still answers, or whether an open decision under a skipped container (D1a's effective skip) is skipped for this purpose.
- Call: a decision contributes its answer only while it is decided and relevant (the same test E3 uses for role fills: "relevant-and-decided"); it contributes `undecided` only while open, relevant, and not effectively skipped; anything else (skipped, effectively skipped, not relevant, or relevance undecided, decided or not) is unanswered. So skipping a container also settles the conditions that read the open decisions inside it, and a node kept under that skip (D1a) whose condition reads such a decision becomes not relevant unless the decision is kept too. Inherited skips depend on stored states and keeps only, so relevance can read them before effective skip itself, which also needs relevance, is resolved.
- Alternatives: an effectively skipped open decision still contributes `undecided` (then its dependents stay undecided until someone keeps or reopens it, which Gating's "nothing stays undecided forever because of a decision nobody will make" argues against); a decided decision under an undecided ancestor still answers (then an answer counts while the decision itself may leave scope).
- What would change it: users keeping a node under a skip and expecting its condition to keep reading a skipped decision as pending.
