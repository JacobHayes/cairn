# A decision answers a condition only while it is decided and relevant

Question: a condition reads a skipped, not-relevant, or undecided decision as unanswered. That leaves open whether a decided decision whose own relevance is undecided still answers, and whether an open decision under a skipped container still counts as undecided.

Call: a decision contributes its answer only while decided and relevant, the same test a role fill uses, and contributes undecided only while open, relevant, and not effectively skipped. Anything else is unanswered. So skipping a container settles the conditions that read the open decisions inside it, and a node kept under that skip whose condition reads such a decision becomes not relevant unless the decision is kept too.

Alternatives: an effectively skipped open decision still counting as undecided (its dependents would wait on a decision nobody will make); a decided decision under an undecided ancestor still answering (its answer would count while the decision may leave scope).

What would change it: people keeping a node under a skip and expecting its condition to keep reading the skipped decision as pending.
