# A stale proposal is refreshed by drafting it again, its choices carried by what they are about

Question: when a proposal's destination has moved, what does refreshing it mean, and what happens to the reviewer's choices?

Call: a save as route is drafted again from the journey as it is now. Each choice on the old items carries to the new item about the same thing (an identity's mapping by identity, an exclusion by node); an item with no counterpart starts at the draft's default. Any other proposal keeps its mutations and items and names the destination's current revision. The title and description stay as edited. A refresh is an ordinary proposal edit, so the proposal's revision moves and an apply naming the revision reviewed before is stale: review is renewed by construction.

Alternatives: refresh in place on apply (applies something nobody reviewed); keep the old items as drafted (they would describe the old base).

What would change it: proposals that must remember how they were drafted beyond their mutations, such as an assistant draft that could be asked again.
