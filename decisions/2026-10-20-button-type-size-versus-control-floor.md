# Buttons and selects are 14px but DESIGN also sets a 15px floor for controls

Question: DESIGN's Buttons entry says Plex Sans 14px, while its accessibility section says rows, controls and inspector text are at least 15px. The UI critique of the index heads flagged every button and select (raised while shaping the shared index head).

Call: left as built. The Buttons entry is the specific rule and every control in the product follows it; changing the shared control size touches every screen and is a design call for the owner, not part of an index-head change.

Alternatives: raise buttons and selects to 15px at the same control height; reword the floor to exempt buttons.

What would change it: the owner choosing 15px controls, then one edit to the shared button and select styles and to the Buttons entry.
