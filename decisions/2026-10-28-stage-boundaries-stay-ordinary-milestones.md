# Stage boundaries stay ordinary milestones

Question: the milestones that open and close a stage could be folded into the stages zoomed out, drawn as bands or arrows between tiles, or drawn as a rule (work: the graph overview, tiles).

Call: a milestone is an ordinary card at every zoom, whatever it opens or closes: drawn where the layout puts it, scaled like every card, with its own date and state, its lines into a stage's tile drawn to the tile like any other line. A stage that has no event to wait for may wait on a stage directly, and takes its own date rule or pin for a deadline.

Alternatives and what each costs: folding boundary milestones zoomed out (a rule for which fold, a joined line, exceptions that draw them anyway; one kind of node would change look as you zoom); bands and a stage-to-stage arrow (a second layout pass, slivers at fit, the arrow removed the milestone even where you act on it); a vertical rule or narrow column (the same special case in another shape). The cost of the call: at a far fit a milestone between two stages is a speck between the tiles, as any small card is, and one zoom step makes it readable.

What would change it: evidence that people lose the order of stages at a far fit.
