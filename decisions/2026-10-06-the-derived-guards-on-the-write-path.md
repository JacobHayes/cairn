# The derived guards on the write path

- Question: D4 lists relevance among the completing guards ("relevant") without saying whether `undecided` passes; ARCHITECTURE runs the guards against one derive of the final candidate but not what happens when the candidate breaks a structural invariant; the brief wants a failure to name the mutation that caused it; a stored bypass outlives the completion it covered; and B6's "transitively depends" does not say over which edges or how often it is checked.
- Call: (1) the relevance guard needs `relevant`: an undecided node does not complete, answer, or reach (it also fails `deps_done` on its deciding decision); force include is the escape hatch. (2) The derived stage runs only when the patch's journey holds every graph invariant (derive asserts them), like the plan check; the structural violations are reported instead. (3) Each failure carries `Violation.caused_by`: the patch's other mutations whose writes shaped the node, the open dependency, or the ancestor, condition, or stage that carries it (structure), the dependency's or a read decision's state, or their overrides. (4) Reopening a node clears its guard bypass, so a recorded failure is never accepted for a later completion. (5) A `deps_done` bypass records each open dependency as `OpenDependency` on the stored bypass and its event, beside 2.1's artifact and breakdown failures. (6) Snooze wait cycles are a graph invariant: each node snooze is a wait from the snoozed node's start to its target's finish over the full set's gate edges, so a target that depends on the snoozed node, is its ancestor, or waits on it through another snooze is rejected on every patch, independent of answers. (7) Beyond the ruling's terminal and not-relevant cases, a snooze on an effectively skipped node is also rejected, since an effective skip is a terminal state for every other purpose.
- Alternatives: (1) `undecided` passing relevance (a node could complete before its condition is decided); (3) naming only the completing mutation; (4) bypasses kept until removed by hand; (6) the pruned set (an answer could then make an accepted snooze illegal, or the reverse) or a check only on snooze mutations (an edge added later could close the cycle unseen).
- What would change it: users who want to complete undecided work without a force include; a need to keep a bypass across a reopen.
