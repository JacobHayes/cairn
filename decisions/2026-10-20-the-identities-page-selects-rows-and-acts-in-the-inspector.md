# The Identities page selects rows and acts in the inspector

- Question: how the Identities page is shaped now that identities are deleted and merged several at a time, and the owner asked for an Add button in the title row, row selection with bulk merge and delete, and no junk or dead space (identities-page).
- Call: the page is the Index head (Add at the right end of the title row, a search and a count in the toolbar row) over a one-line-per-row table. Rows select into the one set of Multi-select (`canvas/selection.ts`, reused as it is): a row or its name opens one identity at `/identities/<key>`; the checkboxes and Shift- or Cmd-click add and remove. Add opens its form in the inspector at `/identities/new`, and on success the new identity is open at its address once the deployment shows it. Every action lives in the inspector: one identity shows Edit, what refers to it (the references read, each a link) and Delete; several show Merge (a Keep choice over the members, the viewer's own by default, then one `merge_identities` naming the journeys the references read gave for all of them) and Delete (one patch, one `delete_identity` per member). Delete is on only when the references read is current for the deployment and lists nothing for every member: a read going, failed, behind or silent about a member leaves it off with the reason in words. Delete is one click, with no typed confirmation: the references read guards it, nothing is lost with it but the identity's name and emails, and the history keeps the event. Saved emails show as plain mono text, not chips (chips are for typing an email and for a filter that is on), which changes DESIGN's Multi-value input sentence. An address no identity answers (deleted or merged away) says so in the inspector. A merge takes at most the count of merged identities the engine allows (`identity_count_per_fill_max`); past it the panel says to remove some.
- Alternatives: keep the always-open form and the two-select merge fold (the owner called both ugly); a typed-name confirmation for Delete as for a journey (the read already refuses everything that matters, and a journey's deletion loses its history); a modal for Add (the inspector is where every other form of the app stands, and its sheet already works on a phone); chips for saved emails (they compete with the "You" mark and read as controls); one read per member in the inspector (a single read of the whole selection answers Delete for all of them at one freshness).
- What would change it: an identity list large enough that a client-side search and the 100-member merge limit get in the way (the deployment holds at most 5,000 identities, so a server-side filter would come first), or a Delete someone regrets, which would call for undo before a confirmation.
