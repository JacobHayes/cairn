# The service and API split at the engine's 2.4 boundary

- Question: 4.1 depended on 2.7, so the service, the API, and everything after them waited on priority, projections, and route files, although most of the service (composition, actors, receipts, loading, commits, conflicts, notification, capabilities, store-backed reads, and journey writes with D7 consequences) needs only what 2.1 to 2.4 and 3.1 provide. A design review, approved by the owner, asked whether they could start now without stubs.
- Call: split both briefs. 4.1 (depending on 2.4 and 3.1) builds the runtime-free composition, actor and verified-email resolution, request-scoped time and deployment inputs, receipt-first idempotency, record loading, commit, precondition, and conflict plumbing, post-commit notification with initial subscription revisions, capabilities, the store-backed resource, index, search, and event reads, and domain patches through `apply` with D7 consequences. A new 4.8 (depending on 2.7 and 4.1) adds priority (2.5), derived reads and every projection with memoization (2.6), and import and export, proposal preview, resolution, and lifecycle, upgrade, save-as-route, and re-link (2.7); it takes I6, D6, the confirming user of H2, and the planted confirming-user bug. 4.2 keeps the endpoints for 4.1's operations, errors, limits, observability, SSE, OpenAPI, the generated clients, and the conflict and subscription logic, and gains `/document` from 2.6 (it packages stored state, the derive inputs, and the engine version, with no derived value); a new 4.9 (depending on 4.8 and 4.2) adds the endpoints that need 4.8. 4.3 and 4.5 now depend on 4.9, since their tools and wasm package need projections and proposals, so only 4.1, 4.2, 6.1, and 6.2 start earlier. Deferred operations do not exist yet: nothing returns "not implemented", and the 4.1 write path takes domain patches only, so a proposal cannot be submitted around its lifecycle. The design review's matching split of 4.5 and 4.6 is not made here.
- Why: parallelism (the service and API proceed beside 2.5 to 2.7), at low risk: the engine interfaces the service builds on (`apply`, `Records`, `ApplyInputs`, `derive`, `consequences`) were not changed by any review round of 2.1 to 2.4.
- Alternatives: wait for 2.7 (phase 4 serialized behind the engine); one 4.1 with stubs for deferred operations (forbidden by AGENTS, No stubs); 4.1 exposing proposal patches through `apply` now (apply does not yet enforce unresolved review items, so it would publish an incomplete proposal API).
- What would change it: 2.5 to 2.7 changing `apply`, `ApplyInputs`, `Records`, `derive`, or `consequences` (4.1's loader and write path would follow, a rework 4.8 absorbs), or 4.8 finding that proposals need a write path other than 4.1's.
