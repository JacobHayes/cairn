# A container shows the gravity of its whole area

Question: containment makes every child's gravity at least its parent's, so a container showing its own gravity beside its heaviest child's shows two numbers, one apparently larger than the whole.

Call: a container shows one number, its subtree gravity: the counted weight of the container and its open, in-scope descendants, plus everything downstream of any of them, each node once, under gravity's pruning and terminal rules. It is at least every member's gravity, a dependent shared by two children counts once, and a container whose work is all done shows 0. Node gravity, unlocks, and rank are unchanged, so a deliverable with children is still ranked on its own gravity.

Alternatives: the peak gravity inside (the number that caused the confusion); the sum of the children's gravities (double counts shared dependents).

What would change it: a screen that wants to point at the heaviest node inside a container.
