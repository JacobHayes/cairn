# A direct fill of a decision-filled role is routed by the client

Question: a role with a filling decision is filled only through that decision, and the engine refuses a direct fill or clear of it, naming the decision. Who turns "fill this role" into the answer?

Call: the client, as it does for a milestone pin a decision feeds. Filling such a role answers its filling decision with the identity or identities; clearing it reopens the decision. An agent reads the decision from the refusal or the graph and does the same. The engine keeps one authority per role and never rewrites a fill into an answer.

Alternatives: the engine rewriting the fill into an answer (one mutation would record another type's event and take on the answer's guards and dates); accepting the direct fill as an override (two authorities per role).

What would change it: a client that cannot read the graph, or a need for the fill and the answer to be one event.
