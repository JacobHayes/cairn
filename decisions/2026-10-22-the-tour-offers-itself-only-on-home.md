# The tour offers itself only on Home

Question: the tour should reach everyone who has not seen it, and a link to specific work must stay useful. Where does it open by itself?

Call: only on Home, after the name prompt has closed and only if the person is still on Home when their record arrives. Everywhere else a quiet link in the rail (while the tour is unfinished) and a section on the user's own page offer it. A deep link to a journey, node, route, proposal or the user's page is never interrupted and nothing is drawn over the destination; a tour left in progress shows one line offering to continue it. Finishing or skipping returns to Home.

Alternatives: a dialog on every index page (more surfaces for little gain); a nonmodal invitation over deep-linked screens (layout work on every screen and on phones); redirecting first visits (breaks links).

What would change it: evidence that new people do not find the rail link or the user's page.
