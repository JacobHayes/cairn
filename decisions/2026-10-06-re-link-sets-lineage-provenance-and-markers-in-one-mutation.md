# Re-link sets lineage, provenance, and markers in one mutation; save-as-route maps entities per participation

- Question: the ruling asks for a re-link mutation that sets the lineage and the markers atomically (B9: preserve the old lineage until apply). The schema's `relink` set only the lineage. B8 leaves open what "set the graph default_owner" does to an entity's participations, and what happens when one participation names entities mapped differently.
- Call: `relink { lineage }` now writes, in its own event, the lineage; for each journey node the version holds, `from_route` provenance and exactly the markers its differences from the version's node call for (set where they differ, cleared where they match); for every other node, `local` provenance and no markers; and the version's roles and kinds the journey lacks. `relink(records, journey, lineage)` drafts that mutation with one conflict per difference, kept by default; taking the route's value writes it after the re-link and clears the marker. Save as route drafts `create_route` (for a new route) or nothing, `open_draft { save_as_route }`, then the journey's structure (roles, kinds, default owner, nodes by path, keys kept, no state) as ordinary mutations, with one participation item per explicit entity and one exclusion item per node (a journey-local child of a placeholder starts excluded). Resolving rewrites the drafted node additions: an excluded node and its subtree are left out with the edges into them; a participation naming explicit entities becomes the one role they map to (the default-owner choice maps to the graph's `default_owner` role), goes when all are dropped, and blocks every involved item when they map to different roles.
- Alternatives: re-link as many mutations (provenance and marker writes per node, past the patch limit at 2,000 nodes with several differences each); the default-owner choice clearing the participation so the node inherits (equal at the root, different under an ancestor with its own owner); exclusion as removals after the additions (would retire the excluded keys in the route).
- What would change it: the review screen (5.7) wanting per-participation rather than per-entity mappings, or re-link offering exclusions of nodes the version holds.
