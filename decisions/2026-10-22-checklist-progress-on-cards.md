# Checklist progress on cards: "7 of 11 ticked" on childless work

Question, from the checklist screens: which one fact does a graph card show for a checklist,
and what when the node also holds other nodes?

Call: work (a deliverable or action) that holds no other nodes shows its checklist as its
one body line, the progress meter and "7 of 11 ticked", in the style of a container's "1 of 3
done". Work that holds children keeps its children's progress; its checklist is in the
inspector only. The count shows from "0 of 11 ticked", so ticking never changes a card's
size or the layout. A done card shows its real count: "11 of 11 ticked", or "7 of 11 ticked"
when it was marked done without them or an item was unticked since. The list and Summary rows
do not carry the count: their one fact says why a row is on the frontier, or "4 checklist
items to tick" when finishing needs ticks.

Alternatives:

- The request's "7/11" with a box glyph: a bare figure breaks the plain-words rule, and a
  box glyph on a card reads as a control.
- Both counts on a container: two facts on one card read as one.
- The count on list rows: crowds out why a row is on the frontier.

What would change it: owners asking to see checklist progress in the list, which would make it
a List column rather than a row fact.
