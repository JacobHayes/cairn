# A proposal's change marks scale with the card they mark

Question (from writing the owner's working principles into the documents): the earlier call, `2026-10-20-a-proposals-changes-are-marked-by-fill-edge-and-the-cards-own-words.md`, made "never a badge" its rule. What failed in review was a tag held at a constant size on screen: zoomed out it outgrew the cards, overlapped their neighbours, and was worse on a phone. The owner's reading is that a badge may be fine and the poor scaling was the problem. Is a badge itself ruled out?

Call: no. A change mark scales with the card it marks, so it reads at every zoom and on a phone and never covers a neighbour; any form that does so is open, a badge included. The marks as built (the fill, the 2px edge, and the change's word leading the card's kind line, which is in effect a badge that scales with the card) meet that rule and stay unchanged. The design language's Proposal layer says the rule ("marked by what scales with the card") instead of "without badges".

Alternatives: keep "never a badge" (it bans a form for a failure of scaling, so later work would argue from the title rather than the reason); rewrite the earlier record in place (records stay as they were written, and a later call supersedes them).

What would change it: a mark that scales with its card and still reads as clutter on a fitted graph or a phone, seen in screenshots.
