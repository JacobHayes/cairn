# Search narrows every view to the engine's matches

Question (from making every journey view share one view state): the graph's search jumped to a card whose title matched. The list searched titles, descriptions, notes and resources and dropped the rest. The walk through had no search. What does search mean on a journey?

Call: search narrows, on every view, through the level request. A node is drawn when its title, description, notes or resources hold the text, or when it holds such a node. The rest roll up as any hidden node does. On the graph the search is part of what the layout is keyed by, so a search refits the canvas to what is left: that is the graph's jump to a match. A search that matches nothing shows an empty view that offers Reset the view.

Alternatives:

- Search as emphasis, fading non-matches: the walk through cannot emphasise within one card, and a list that fades its non-matches does not find anything.
- Keep the graph's title-only find beside the shared search: two meanings for one box.

What would change it: journeys too large for a refit to read as finding, which would call for a jump between matches within the narrowed graph.
