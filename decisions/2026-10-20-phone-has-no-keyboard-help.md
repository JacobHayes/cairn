# The phone's top bar has no keyboard help and no You tab

Question: should the phone's top bar fold You and the "?" keyboard help into a menu (from the owner's review of Home on a phone)?

Call: no menu. On a phone the "?" help is not offered (there is no keyboard to press its keys), and "You" is reached from the sync dot's popover, which the top bar already carries. Folding them into a separate "..." menu would add a second control beside the sync dot for one link.

Alternatives: a "..." menu holding You and the help; a You tab in the bar (the owner found it odd).

What would change it: a second account-level destination (settings, sign out) that needs a home on a phone, or phones with a keyboard being a real use.
