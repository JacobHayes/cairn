# One owner phrase, one list phrase, one pipeline for the views, and what a narrow list gives up

- Question: the same facts were worded and wired once per surface (owners, node links, titles of removed nodes, plurals and lists of names, the level pipeline of seven views, the done and tone styling), and the inspector named only the first owner (shared-node-parts).
- Call: `ownerWords` says every owner by name, the viewer as "You" (joined with the others), and "Unassigned" for none, on cards, rows, the list, the Summary, Home and the inspector. `namedList` is the one list of names: up to three whole, past that two and "and N more" (never "and 1 more"), unbounded where a name must not be dropped (History). The built-in owner kind is named "Owner" everywhere, an untitled role or kind by its id and a removed one in words (`slotName`), and a merged person is followed through aliases in proposal review too. `useViewLevel` is the one wiring of depth, drawn step, level request, level, Frontier lighting, Depth's Fade and the badges; the walk through is the same pipeline with Fade off and its candidates as what Frontier lights, and every view now counts what Depth hides against the containers the depth collapses (the list and timeline counted against none). One `NodeLink` (title as its tooltip, an optional `to`), one `NodeItem` for rows, one kind list in the order the toggles show, status and tone styling as shared `[data-state]` and `.ink-bad`/`.ink-warn` rules. In the list, below 810px of region (a phone, or a window with the inspector open) the Owner column goes with the columns that give way, since the title needs about 330px to keep a row's buttons on its line and the inspector says the owner; on a phone the tree stops indenting after two levels.
- Alternatives: capping owners at three in the inspector (it exists to say who, in full); keeping Owner at the cost of a title column too narrow for its buttons (a row then reserved a blank second line); a depth marker beside a capped phone indent (the guides and the Contents of a group already say where a row sits).
- What would change it: owners on a list with the inspector open mattering more than the title's room (then a narrower owner column showing initials), or journeys with so many owners per node that the full phrase needs a cap.
