# Proof pages are no longer committed

- Question (from the owner's call on repository size): AGENTS asked every change someone can see or use to commit a proof page with screenshots and video under `briefs/proof/`. Should it still?
- Call: no. A change someone can see is shown by its screenshots, and the screen critique in review judges them; the builder still takes a picture of every state the change touches and fixes what looks off. The screenshots, videos, specs and notes stay in the change's scratch directory, outside the repository, and nothing lands under `briefs/proof/`. The existing pages stay as history. `mise run screenshots` and `mise run proof` stay, for taking those pictures, with their output outside version control. The proof pages had grown to 85 MB of a 253 MB repository pack and were not read; the critique, run on the pictures, is what caught problems.
- Alternatives: smaller pages (fewer shots, shorter video: still media in history on every visible change, and still unread); keeping pages for videos only (the same cost for the part least read); deleting the existing pages (they record what each milestone shipped, and the owner asked to keep them).
- What would change it: a reader who needs to see what a past change looked like and cannot recover it from the code and the screenshot walk, which would argue for keeping a small set of current screens in the repository rather than one page per change.
