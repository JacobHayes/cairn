# A proposal is reviewed as a layer over its destination, not on a screen of its own

Question (from the proposal-layer unit): a proposal had its own review screen with its own Graph | List switcher, filter chips, a head and a trail, so the same journey looked different there than on its page. Where is a proposal reviewed, and at what address?

Call:

- Reviewing is the destination's own page with a layer on: a journey's Graph, List or Timeline, a route's, or Entities' rows. The header, the View picker and the shared view controls are the page's own, and the views draw the destination as it would be after the proposal. There is no review screen and no View picker entry for it.
- The paths stay as PRD C14 has them: `/journeys/<id>/proposals/<pid>`, `/routes/<id>/proposals/<pid>`, `/entities/proposals/<pid>`, with `/nodes/<key>` below them for journeys and routes only (the deployment has no nodes, so its node path is removed). The query is the destination page's own. The layer opens on the Graph on every width, a phone included (a bare journey opens on the Summary, which cannot carry the layer); `view=summary` and `view=walk` under a proposal resolve to the Graph. Links into and out of the layer are built from the view on screen, so entering from the List keeps the List and Close writes the view explicitly when it differs from the bare destination's default. Rows of the Proposals tab keep the page's query. Close, Apply and Discard return to the destination's address; a proposal that creates its destination returns to where it came from (its source journey or route, else the index), and Apply lands on the new destination.
- The layer adds one line under the header, the band: the title, Apply and Close. Discard is at the rail's foot. The rail is the Inspector tab's content while the layer is on; the Proposals tab lists the destination's open proposals everywhere, Entities included, with the open one marked current. Picking a card or row opens its item editor in the rail.
- While the layer is on the page offers no live write, because acting on a picture of the future would be wrong: node actions, authoring and structure tools, the header's write items, the graph's context menu, multi-select. The proposal's own writes remain. The Walkthrough and the Summary stay in the View picker, disabled with their reason.
- A proposal that is not open shows the destination without marks and the band saying so, with its title, proposer and changes in the rail, read only. A missing proposal shows the page and a band saying so.

Alternatives: a View picker entry (rejected: the review is not a way of looking at the destination); keeping the screen and linking to it (the views keep differing); a query flag `?proposal=<pid>` on the bare address (rejected: PRD C14 names the paths); opening on the List on a phone (the graph is the page, and panning it on a phone is wanted).

What would change it: a destination whose views cannot draw a candidate (a view that has no honest after).
