# Applying a proposal takes one click, at the revision on screen

**Question.** A proposal edited since it was reviewed must be reviewed again. What makes Apply mean "I reviewed this"?

**Call.** No confirmation step. Apply is one click whenever nothing holds it, and it sends as the reviewed revision the proposal revision whose preview the page has finished drawing, never the newest one read. While the drawn preview is not the latest, Apply is held until it is redrawn; an edit that lands between press and commit is refused by the server's revision check, and the page redraws. Review again is the reviewer seeing the refreshed proposal drawn.

**Alternatives.** An "I have reviewed this" checkbox (a formality that a refresh asks for again); a stored record of who reviewed which revision.

**What would change it.** A requirement that review be recorded per person, which would move it to the host.
