# Inline resolution sends the rejected edit and the chosen move as one patch; a fed milestone unpins by reopening its decision; designating an artifact retypes the link

- Question: F5 says the UI presents resolution "inline against the rejected patch", and each `ShortChain` resolution is "an ordinary mutation that, applied alone, gives this chain the days it lacks"; it does not say whether choosing one sends the move alone or with the edit. E3 says "an inline `unpin` routes through the decision" without naming the mutation. G2 says a deliverable "can designate one or more links as its artifact(s)" while the schema types each link (artifact, reference, conversation).
- Call: (1) choosing a move sends one patch: the rejected patch's mutations followed by the move, on the current revision, so the edit is kept and the chain given its days (a `shift_pin` after the `set_pin` moves the new pin; an `answer` moves the other end); if that is rejected again, its rejection shows in the same place. A shortfall in the derived state (F6, no rejected edit) sends the move alone. (2) Pinning a milestone a `feeds_milestone` decision feeds answers that decision with the date; unpinning it reopens the decision (D1: reopen empties what the decision drove). (3) Designating a link as the artifact edits it to an `artifact` link with the same key, node, title, and address; undesignating makes it a `reference`.
- Alternatives: (1) sending the move alone (it resolves a chain that was never stored, so it is either a no-op or a different edit than the person made); (2) a direct `clear_pin` on a fed milestone (the engine rejects it as `pinned_through_decision`); (3) a separate designation flag on links (no such field; the type already says it), or keeping a designated conversation's type on undesignation (the schema has no memory of it).
- What would change it: a resolution that is several mutations (DECISIONS.md 2.3), or links that must keep a conversation type through designation.
