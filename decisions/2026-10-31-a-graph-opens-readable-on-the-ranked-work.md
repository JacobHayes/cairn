# A graph opens readable on the work ranked first

Question: when a journey's graph opens, does it fit the whole graph whatever zoom that takes, or open where its cards can be read?

Call: the opening view frames, in order, the acting frontier's cards by rank and then the whole graph, at no less than the readable zoom: the first-ranked card always, each later box while everything framed still reads. A graph that reads whole opens whole, as Fit shows it; a larger one opens on the work ranked first with what is next to it. A proposal's layer opens the same way on its changes. With nothing on the frontier, or none of it drawn, the view fits the whole graph. The Fit button always shows the whole graph.

Alternatives: fitting the whole graph always (on a phone the fixtures opened at a tenth of full size, cards and tiles too small for their titles); a phone-only floor (a second opening rule for one width, while a desktop's whole fit is no more legible below the same zoom); opening on the active stage (a container can be larger than the window, and the frontier is what the person acts on).

What would change it: people who open a journey to survey it more often than to act on it.
