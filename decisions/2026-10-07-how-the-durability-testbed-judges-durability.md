# How the durability testbed judges durability

- Question: under a crash-restart the process loses its memory, so how does the restarted incarnation know which commits were acknowledged, and how are the PRACTICES invariants checked?
- Call: the client keeps a ledger beside the store (`testbeds/durability/src/ledger.rs`): the plan's seed first, then an intent before each submission, the commit points the store's fault hook reports, and an acknowledgement with its revision after each answer, each entry checksummed and synced before the client acts on it, a torn last entry cut off on reopen. The plan is a pure function of its seed, so a restart rebuilds every patch. After every restart, after every in-process reopen, and at the end, an audit loads the whole log and checks four invariants, each a `violation` verdict and exit 1: every acknowledged step has its events and its receipt at the acknowledged revision; a commit's receipt and all its events are present together or not at all; the log holds only planned, submitted steps, once each, as a prefix of the plan; and every journey and the deployment load equal to the engine's replay of the log from empty (J3). The step in flight at a crash is resubmitted and must be answered from its receipt exactly when it landed (H5). A store that fails to open on an erroring disk is a pass with `outcome=unavailable`: an honest answer with nothing to judge; the crash sweep injects no errors and requires every run to complete, which is where a store that cannot open after a crash would fail. The service runs as a composition root assembles it, and a failed open is not retried in-process.
- Alternatives: `always!` for the invariants (the brief's wording; its abort leaves no trace for a crash-restart run, 6.1's patina entry); keeping acknowledgements outside the simulated disk (patina offers no channel that survives the restart); a crash schedule drawn by the guest (patina's crash selector is the only crash that tears writes).
- What would change it: a patina channel that survives a restart, or a campaign that draws crash points (then the sweep folds into the campaign).
