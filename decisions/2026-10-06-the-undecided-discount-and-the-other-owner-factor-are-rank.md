# The undecided discount and the other-owner factor are rank constants, and scores are exact millionths

- Question: the PRD's Priority formulas write the undecided discount (0.5) and the cross-owner factor (2) inline, its Open questions list both among the rank constants "to tune against the seed route", and ARCHITECTURE makes the rank constants configuration; but the schema's `RankConstants` (1.2) held only the blend and the urgency horizon, and `Score` counted half-weights, exact only for a discount of 0.5. The brief asks for an exact representation that supports the configured discount.
- Call: `RankConstants` gains `undecided_discount` (0 to 1, default 0.5) and `other_owner_factor` (1 to `weight_max`, default 2), each counted in thousandths (`Thousandths<MIN, MAX>`, written as its value, rejected off the 0.001 grid or out of range). The factor's bound is the nearest named limit, as the limits table names none. `Score` is a `u64` count of millionths of a weight: each term (whole weight x discount x factor in thousandths) is a whole number of millionths, so gravity and leverage are exact sums; at the limits a sum is at most 2 x 10^15, under `Score::MAX` (2^51), where a value written as a JSON number reads back exactly. Rank terms are floats.
- Alternatives: the discount and factor fixed in the formula with half-weight scores (the PRD and the brief call them tunable); float gravity (sums would depend on order and break "never decreases upstream" by an ulp); scores written as integer millionths on the wire (exact at any size, but every client would divide).
- What would change it: a tuning that needs finer than thousandths, or a factor past `weight_max`.
