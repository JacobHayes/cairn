# Blockers and stale reasons are listed once, on demand

- Question: the coordinator's ruling forbids materializing inherited blockers per descendant (up to nodes x depth x 64 entries), but the schema's `NodeDerived.blocked_by` and `stale` would do exactly that for an ancestor's open requirement.
- Call: `NodeDerived.blocked_by` holds the node's own unsatisfied dependencies (its explicit requirements, condition gates, stage opening, and children), and a new `blocked_through` names the ancestors, nearest first, whose own unsatisfied requirements, conditions, or openings block it; each lists them in its own `blocked_by`, even when that ancestor is closed or not relevant itself (a skipped container holding back kept work, a not-relevant container holding back a force include). The engine computes both on demand from the entry chains (`Derived::blocked_by`, `Derived::blocked_through`), and stores per node only whether it is stale; `Derived::stale(graph, key)` lists the reasons (open dependencies, inherited ones included, as `OpenDependency`) when asked. `consequences` therefore takes both sides' graphs as well as their `Derived` values (ARCHITECTURE, Write path, says so).
- Alternatives: `Explained<Blocker>` per node with every inherited blocker (the materialization the ruling forbids); a `Blocker` enum with an ancestor variant (consumers would have to filter the ancestor's containment blockers back out).
- What would change it: a surface that needs every descendant's full blocker list at once, which would argue for a paged listing endpoint rather than a stored list.
