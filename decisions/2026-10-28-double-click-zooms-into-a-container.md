# Double-click zooms into a container

Question: with containers drawn as tiles zoomed out, what should a double-click, a double-tap or Enter on a container do (work: the graph overview, tiles)?

Call: zoom into it, framed at least at the zoom it opens at, so it is drawn open and its contents are readable. Folding is the fold control's alone. A collapsed container has nothing to zoom into, so the gestures expand it, as before.

Alternatives: keep a double-click folding (a tile that folds is a gesture that changes the graph's depth while the intent is to look closer); add a canvas control for zooming into a stage (a new control for what a double-click already says).

What would change it: people double-clicking a collapsed container expecting it to frame rather than expand.
