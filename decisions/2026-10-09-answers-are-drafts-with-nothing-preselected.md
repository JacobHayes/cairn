# Answers are drafts with nothing preselected, and a new answer starts with no reason

Question: how the answer form treats a default choice and the reason given with an earlier answer.

Call: picking an option is a draft, nothing is preselected, and Save is enabled only when the answer or reason differs from what is stored. Choosing a different answer empties the reason and offers the previous one to reuse, so copying it is deliberate; editing only the reason of the current answer starts from its text. If someone else answers mid-draft, the form offers keeping or discarding the draft.

Alternatives: a preselected default (hides that a decision is open); carrying the reason forward (attaches a reason to an answer it was not written for).

What would change it: decisions whose answers are usually refinements of the last, where the old reason as an editable start would serve better.
