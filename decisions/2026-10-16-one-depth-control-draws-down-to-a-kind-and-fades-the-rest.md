# One Depth control draws down to a kind, and Fade dims the rest

Question (from the work that folded the Detail ladder and the Decisions button into one control, PRD C2, C11, C12): the toolbar had a Detail ladder (Stages, Decisions, Work, All) and a Decisions button that meant four different things (a graph fade, list rows, walk cards, a timeline highlight). What is the one control, what do its steps mean on each view, and what stays of the old button?

Call:

- **One control, Depth, of four steps named after the deepest kind each draws:** Stages, Decisions, Deliverables, Actions. They are the ladder's steps renamed (Work becomes Deliverables, All becomes Actions), one table shared by every view, keys 1 to 4. The address parameter is `depth=` (steps `stages`, `decisions`, `deliverables`, `actions`; Actions is the default and leaves the address bare). The old `detail=` and `decisions=` parameters are read nowhere and have no alias; nothing stores them. Each step means "draw down to this kind; everything deeper rolls up into its container", always through the engine's level request, so roll-ups and the hidden-prerequisites marker hold at every step.
- **Stages keeps its exception:** it draws the decisions and deliverables of the current stages and leaves out top-level non-stages (PRD C5), so the ladder is additive from Decisions down, and Stages is the one step that is not a plain kind set.
- **Fade is a checkbox in the Depth menu**, "Fade the rest instead of hiding it", off by default and kept in the address as `fade=1`. With it on every view draws the Actions level and dims the nodes Hide at the chosen depth would not draw as their own card, at DESIGN's 0.3. Its layout key is the Actions one, so stepping the depth with Fade on moves nothing, and a viewer's collapsed containers stay collapsed. Toggling Fade at a shallower depth adds cards, so that layout (the Actions one, already cached when the viewer came from Actions) is the one drawn, and the camera refits as it does whenever the drawn set changes. Hide returns to the depth's own cached layout. The walk through and the Summary show one card at a time or no cards, so the walk's Depth menu has no checkbox and the Summary has no Depth. Reset the view keeps Frontier and Fade (neither hides anything).
- **Depth with Fade is not exactly the old Decisions button.** The old button also dimmed milestones and groups holding no decision; Decisions depth draws those, so they stay at full strength. What dims is what Hide would not draw. A container holding a node in focus (a decision may sit inside a deliverable or an action) is not dimmed, but only when that node is itself drawn by the level, so a filter or relevance class that hides it ends the exemption. Frontier dimming and Depth dimming combine as a union of the sets they dim, per card or row: a card out of either focus is 0.3, never 0.3 times 0.3.
- **The walk through at Decisions depth walks decisions only** (the decision walkthrough, PRD C11): the level at Decisions also draws milestones, and a new journey's kickoff milestone is on the frontier from the start and would be walked first. At the other steps it walks the frontier items the step draws. A new journey still opens on `walk?depth=decisions`, and its "Continue with everything" goes back to Actions depth.
- **The list at Decisions depth shows the decision columns** (Answer, Why, Affects, after Title) on the tree of groups, decisions and milestones; rows of other kinds leave them blank, headers stay Title and Due, and the count says "items". Columns no longer offers the three while they are shown, and keeps Kind (the rows are mixed).
- **Kind picking is gone from every Filter** (already so on the journey views); the Filter keeps mine, owner, flags, not relevant and conditional.
- **The rule in PRD C2 and DESIGN** reads: Depth, Filter and search choose what is drawn; Frontier, and Depth with Fade, only emphasise. The ticket worded the second half as "Frontier and search emphasise"; search stays a narrowing on every view, as the earlier call that a search narrows every view to the engine's matches records (a walk through cannot emphasise one card, and a long list that fades its non-matches finds nothing), so this call does not reopen that one.

Why a single choice, not per-kind checkboxes: kinds nest (stages contain deliverables, deliverables contain actions), so an arbitrary set of kinds, such as hidden parents with shown children, has no coherent meaning. The ladder only ever adds the next layer down, so every step is coherent. The per-kind checkbox row the ladder replaced is in `briefs/proof/5.2/` (screenshots 2 and 3).

Alternatives:

- Keep `detail=` and the old step ids, renaming only the words: two names for one thing.
- Dim by kind only under Fade: a Stages fade would leave top-level deliverables and the contents of non-current stages bright, unlike Hide.
- Walk what the level draws at every step, decisions included: one rule, but a new journey would open its decision walkthrough on a milestone.
- Decision rows only on the list at Decisions: that is a kind filter, which this call moves out of every Filter.
- Per-kind checkboxes, kept behind the menu: the confusion the ladder was made to remove.
- A "faded" word or a short description on the Depth trigger, and a description under each step: DESIGN has state show as a fixed-size badge, never text, so no control changes width; the step names are the ticket's, and the tooltip says what Fade does.
- Disabling the Fade checkbox at Actions, where there is nothing deeper to dim: the ticket omits it only on the walk and the Summary, and keeping it enabled keeps the setting when the viewer steps up again.

What would change it: journeys so large that Stages still draws too many cards (a further roll-up step above it), or a use for Fade on the walk through (a queue that shows what is behind the card, say).
