# The timeline's end anchor is the final milestone alone; without one the timeline ends after its latest date

Question: the timeline's end anchor is the final milestone, but a graph may have none, it may have no date yet, and dates may fall after it.

Call: the anchor is the final milestone at its effective date, as the engine's timeline projection names it, and nothing stands in for it. Dates after it are drawn on a shaded stretch past the end, never cut. With no dated final milestone there is no anchor, the page says so, and the axis ends after the latest date.

Alternatives: cutting the axis at the anchor (hides real dates); a stand-in such as the latest milestone (presents as the end a node the route never declared one).

What would change it: a rule that a journey without a final milestone still has a named end, which would go in the engine's projection so every client shares it.
