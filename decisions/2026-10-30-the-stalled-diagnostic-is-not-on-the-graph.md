# The stalled diagnostic is not on the graph

Question: where does a journey that cannot move say what it waits on?

Call: on the Summary, the List and the walk through, which are read top to bottom; not in the graph's head. The graph is a gesture surface that fills what its head leaves, and a diagnostic of any length there takes the room the graph is for. The graph shows the same facts on the cards: what is snoozed, until when, and what is blocked.

Alternatives: the diagnostic in the graph's head (on a phone it pushed the graph out of the window); folded to one line (a second form of the same panel); in the journey card in the inspector (it would move with the selection).

What would change it: a stalled state the cards cannot show.
