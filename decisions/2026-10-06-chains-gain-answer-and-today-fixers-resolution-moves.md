# Chains gain answer and today fixers, resolution moves as mutations, and milestone effective dates

- Question: the schema's chain types had no way to end a chain at today or at a date answer, no instant for a date answer a rule measures from, no type for F5's resolution moves ("as data"), and no field for a milestone's effective date (F1, F7).
- Call: `Instant::Answer { decision }` (a date decision's answer, apart from its decided instant); `FixedBy::Answer` and `FixedBy::Today`; `ShortChain::resolutions: Vec<Mutation>`, each one ordinary mutation that alone gives the chain its missing days: `shift_pin` and `clear_pin` on a direct pin at either end (moved earlier at the chain's start, later at its end), `answer` with the moved date for a pin a feeding decision gives or a date answer, `set_node_field` of a `due_by` or `not_before` with its offset loosened by the shortfall (the direction flips past zero; within the offset limit), `set_node_field` of an estimate shrunk by the shortfall where it is at least that, `remove_edge` for an explicit requirement on the chain (the ancestor's, when inherited), and `set_node_field` `closes: false` for a stage close. Restructuring beyond dropping an edge (splitting work, running it in parallel) is left to the person. `NodeDates::effective_date` with its origin (actual, pin, due). An entry hop is folded into the one `Dependency` it carries, named through its ancestor (`Inherited`, `Condition`, `StageOpening`), so `Instant` needs no entry.
- Alternatives: a dedicated resolution enum (a second vocabulary for the same patches, which the UI and agents would translate back); listing only pin moves.
- What would change it: a client needing a move that is not one mutation.
