# Sign-in has no linking, no auto-link, and no direct Tailscale mode

- Question: the five sign-in providers took about 9.4k lines with their tests; what can go without losing something a deployment uses?
- Call: a login that no user holds creates a user, and people who sign in two ways meet in their identity, found by verified email, so a second sign-in method needs no link step: the browser-linking flow (sign in with another provider while signed in) and the `auto_link` setting of each provider are gone, as is the store's lookup of logins by email. A session cookie that no longer names a live session is ignored and cleared by the challenge, not refused. The Tailscale provider has one mode, proxy, with a required `trusted_proxies`; the direct mode (asking tailscaled whois over its socket) and the `mode` and `socket` settings are gone, and the provider no longer needs to know the listener. The authorize-request checks are shared by the built-in OAuth server and the IAP relay.
- Alternatives: keeping linking for deployments with several providers (the identity already joins them, and the common case is one provider); keeping direct mode for tailnet-only hosts (no deployment runs it, and `tailscale serve` plus `trusted_proxies = ["127.0.0.1"]` covers the same host).
- What would change it: a deployment that needs one user across logins whose emails differ, which would call for an explicit link step again.
