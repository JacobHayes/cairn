## Goal

The derive passes that decide what is in scope and who is involved: three-valued relevance with its producer, the effective dependency graph of entries, starts, and finishes with every implicit edge tagged by source, effective skip and kept work, and participation with `unassigned` and the membership-loss flag. Also the `Derived` struct and the `derive` entry point that later briefs add their passes to.

## Governing

- PRD: The three relationships; Containment; Gating (relevance, Kleene evaluation, force include); A5; B10 (membership-loss flag); D1a (effective skip); D3, D6; E1, E2, E5; F4 (the gating half); Invariants (the cycle check over implicit edges).
- ARCHITECTURE: Engine > Read path: derive, passes 1 to 3 (including the start-and-finish graph); Conditions.
- PRACTICES: Bounds and assertions (no recursion); Back-of-the-envelope first; Code conventions (deterministic by construction).

## Scope

In: the relevance pass; the effective dependency graph, full structural and relevance-pruned, built as ARCHITECTURE describes so an ancestor's requirement reaches descendants through the chain of container entries and is never copied; a validation stage extending 2.1's cycle check to the full structural graph; effective skip with kept work; the participation pass; `Derived` and `derive` holding only passes that exist; cost statements at the limits in each module doc.

Out: dates (2.3); blocking, frontier, snooze, `stale`, derived guards (2.4); priority (2.5); projections (2.6).

## Acceptance

- Behavior by id: Gating (each of the three decision placements yields the stated relevance; `not (x equals yes)` is true when x is skipped; force include drops condition gates and keeps explicit edges), Containment (a child is reached by its parent's requirement through the entry chain, and the explanation names the parent; a child with an estimate fits between its parent's entry and start with no cycle), A5, B10, D1a (skip through containment only; `keep` exempts a subtree; reopen restores inherited skips but not explicit ones), E2 (each resolution step, including explicit empty), E5, F4 (`gates: false`).
- Force include: a force-included child of a container gated by a condition is not held by that condition, but still waits for the container's explicit requirements.
- Edge types: a `gates: false` stage opening holds dates but never blocks; a container whose entry and start are reached is not complete until its own finish.
- Reference test: on generated trees within the limits, reachability over the entry chain equals reachability over the plain expansion that copies each inherited requirement onto every descendant (2.3 and 2.4 extend the same test to date bounds and blocking).
- A date-only cycle (a `gates: false` opening whose milestone requires the group) is accepted; the acyclicity check reads gate edges only.
- Properties: relevance is never undecided when every referenced decision is terminal; every implicit edge names a source; the pruned graph is a subgraph of the full one; no accepted graph has a cycle among gate edges in the full graph; edge count is O(nodes + explicit edges) on generated deep trees.
- Planted bug: drop the child-waits-for-parent-entry edge and show the scenario asserting the test plan's child actions wait for environment access fail.
- Fixtures: the vendor evaluation journey at each decision point yields the relevance and owners its README states.

## Decision log

Appended by the implementer.

- Layout: `cairn_engine::derive` holds `derive` and the engine's `Derived` (one accessor per pass: `relevance`, `dependencies`, `skips`, `participation`), with a module per pass (`relevance`, `dependencies` with `listing` and `cycles`, `skip`, `participation`) and a private `condition` (Kleene evaluation). The schema's `Derived` is not built yet ([`decisions/2026-10-06-derive-returns-the-engines-own-derived-growing-a-field-per.md`](../decisions/2026-10-06-derive-returns-the-engines-own-derived-growing-a-field-per.md)).
- Effective graph layout: nodes by dense index in key order; four instant slots per node (start, finish, requirement entry, condition entry; the entries carry edges only on containers); edges sorted by dependent instant with offsets, plus edge positions sorted by requirement instant, so `waits` and `waited_by` are slices; one pruned flag per edge. Each edge carries an `EdgeSource` (work, entry, chain, containment, explicit, condition, stage opening) and an `EdgeClass` (gate, date-only); `Dependencies::of` turns the chain walk into the schema's `DependencyVia` per dependency, naming the ancestor, the condition's node, or the stage.
- Relevance order: Kahn's algorithm over each node's inputs (its parent and the decisions its condition reads) rather than a plain top-down walk, since a condition may read a decision anywhere outside its subtree; validation's acyclic gate graph guarantees the order. The producer is the nearest node whose condition yields the combined value; below a force include, the force is named.
- Answers in effect (`Relevances::answer_in_effect`): decided and relevant. Role members (E3) and conditions read it; 2.3 reads it for `feeds_milestone` pins.
- Kept work is listed per skipped container (stored or effective): every kept, in-scope node beneath it, nested keeps included (review round 1: a kept node's own state need not cover a kept child). Whether that work satisfies dependencies ("kept work pending") needs `deps_done` and is 2.4's; no schema field was added.
- A route graph derives as a fresh journey would (every node in its kind's initial state); `derive` asserts a journey holds state for every node or none.
- The cycle violation's message lists each edge on the cycle by source (explicit, condition, stage opening, containment, chain), at most `explanation_entry_count_max` of them; its related subjects are the cycle's nodes.
- The relevance guard on completing transitions stays with 2.4's derived guards (as `stages/guards.rs` says), with `deps_done`.
- Generated journeys for derive's property tests (`testing::generated::arb_journey`) are built as documents, not through apply: nodes in depth-first preorder, requirements, condition decisions, and gating openings only on nodes whose subtree ends before the dependent begins, which keeps every gate edge forward in time; non-gating openings may point anywhere outside the group, so date-only cycles occur. Each graph is checked by `Graph::new`. A fixed 2,000-node, depth-16 build with about 45,000 explicit edges checks the edge count stays linear (`dependencies.rs`).
- J3 matrix entries added for scenarios that now exist: the vendor evaluation's decision points (fixtures/README.md states the values), a group skip cascade with kept work and a waiting dependent, an empty stage with its open gate, and role changes re-deriving participations. "Gated decisions answered out of order" waits for 2.4's `deps_done` guard on answers; the blocking halves of the skip and empty-group entries are 2.4's.
- Planted bug: removing the child's link to its parent's requirement entry in `derive/dependencies.rs` fails `a_childs_actions_wait_for_their_parents_requirement` (`thread 'a_childs_actions_wait_for_their_parents_requirement' panicked at crates/engine/tests/dependencies.rs:68:9`, 4 of 7 failing); restored, all pass (`briefs/proof/2.2/README.md`, generated by `prove.sh`). The reference property fails on the same removal.
- Review round 1 (`gpt-6.1-sol`), one finding, valid, fixed with a regression test (`nested_keeps_are_each_kept_work`): kept work stopped at the first kept ancestor, so a kept child of a done kept node, or a force-included kept child of a not-relevant kept node, dropped out of a skipped container's kept work. Every kept, in-scope node beneath a skipped container is now listed.
- Review round 2 (`gpt-6.1-sol`): no findings.
