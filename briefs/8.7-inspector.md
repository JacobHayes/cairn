## Goal

Make node detail the one place a node is read and acted on, with the form as the display. Today a decision hides its question behind a fold and an Answer button, the header prints stored state in green whatever it is, provenance crowds the top, and every section is open. The inspector leads with a header, one plain sentence saying what the node's state means, and its one primary action or form; everything else is a folded row. The same component renders the Next page's cards (8.9), so one layout and one set of actions work everywhere.

## Governing

- PRD: C8 (everything node detail shows), D8 (display state and its secondary detail), B2 (answering, revising, rationale, reopen), D4 (warnings and guard bypasses), B5 (overrides), B6 (snooze and unsnooze), C11 (per-kind actions), E1 (assign inline), G1, G4 (notes), J4 (history).
- ARCHITECTURE: Web UI (Data flow: composed in the tab; history is the one host read; Previews; Drafts survive a reload).
- DESIGN: Approachable by default (detail on demand); Typography (node title); Components (Status, Popover and menu, Dates).
- PRACTICES: Code conventions.

## Scope

In:
- Header: the kind and breadcrumb (each crumb a link) with the display-state chip at the top right; the title in the h2 face, sentence case, renamed in place; one meta line (owner, or an inline Assign for in-scope unfinished leaves; the one most actionable date in words; the rank only when on the acting frontier); flags only when present. An orphaned node shows "Orphaned" with Keep and Remove. Provenance moves to a folded Origin section.
- The sentence, built in the tab from data it already has: ready ("Ready. Ranks #2: it gates 6 nodes and is due in 5 days."), active (since when; "started early, still waits on X"), blocked ("Waiting on X (owner) and the Y stage opening"), conditional ("May not apply: depends on X"; pending adds "which can't be answered until Y is" and "not counted in priority until then"), scheduled, snoozed (until when, through which container, with Unsnooze), done (by whom, when; stale and "may not apply" when they hold), skipped (reason), not relevant ("Doesn't apply because X = no", plus the recorded state kept).
- One primary action and at most one visible secondary by kind and state: a decision's answer form with Save; Mark done (Start beside it when todo); Done... when a note or link is required, opening the field in the action row and sending note and completion in one patch; Break down... (Mark atomic) for a placeholder; Mark reached with a date field defaulting to today; Confirm reached now when scheduled; Unsnooze (or Unsnooze <container>) beside the normal primary when snoozed; on a container holding its own snooze, a line above the actions: "Starting this lifts the snooze on X and its N open items". A blocked decision shows its question and options with Save disabled and "Waits on X"; Answer anyway... in the overflow menu enables it with a required reason. Done, skipped, and not-relevant nodes have no primary.
- Overflow menus listing only what applies, grouped by frequency (snooze, skip, reopen, guard bypasses, include anyway; rename, assign, weight, dates, break down; show in graph, edit node, reset to route, copy link). Skip, Snooze, Keep, Include anyway, and Done anyway open a small inline form in place of the action row, never a modal. Snooze opens a popover: what to set aside (the node, or an ancestor container with its open count, once 7.4 lands) and until when.
- The answer form: description, prompt, help, then the input itself (segmented yes/no, segmented or one select for single choice, checkboxes or a popover for multi choice, a date input, an entity combobox ending in "New person", a growing text input); nothing preselected; a draft until Save, which is enabled only when the draft differs and survives a reload; Save change on a decided decision; WHY (the rationale field, once 7.2 lands); when someone else answers mid-draft, a callout replacing Save: "Ben answered Partner just now" with Use mine and Discard mine; after Save, 8.5's receipt.
- Sections, each a folded row showing its name and at most a count, remembered per viewer: Contents (containers; open), Connections (waits on, unblocks when done, relevance with Include anyway; open when blocked, stale, or conditional), Guidance (open when actionable), Notes and links (open when any exist), Dates (open on shortfall or overdue), People, Origin, History (each answer with its own reason). The edge card (both ends as links with Go to each, its sources) and the container view (roll-up sentence, Contents with hover actions).
- Second part, with 8.2 and 8.3: each choice's effect beside it ("brings in 6", "drops 3", "2 decided later", "pins Kickoff"); a preview sentence once the draft differs, from the existing draft-patch preview, carrying D7's warnings before you commit; the Affects section (nodes by choice, linked), replacing the affects table 8.6 moved here; Why this rank (the sentence, the four rank terms with words, the gravity breakdown with its conditional share by decision and the configured discount, Unblocks now and Still waiting elsewhere, subtree gravity on containers, the formulas behind How).

Out: Undo on the receipt (later, additive); the cards projection and pass rail (8.9); the rationale's storage (7.2).

## Acceptance

- Behavior by id: C8, D8, B2 (answer a boolean with no Answer click; nothing preselected; Save disabled until a change), D4 (Answer anyway asks for a reason and saves through the bypass), B6 (a snoozed container shows the lift-snooze line beside Start), G4 (Done... on a requires-note node completes in one patch), J4 (History reachable at the end of a long inspector).
- Playwright: the overflow menu on a decided decision lists only Reopen and the shape group; a not-relevant node shows its sentence and recorded state with no form; screenshots of a decision (ready, decided, blocked, not relevant), a deliverable (ready, note required), a container, a milestone, and the edge card; with the second part, per-choice effects and Why this rank.
- Planted bug: preselect Yes on a boolean, and show the no-preselect test fail.
