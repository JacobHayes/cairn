# 7.4: Snoozing a container

| | |
|---|---|
| Id | 7.4 |
| Crates or packages | `crates/schema`, `crates/engine`, `crates/service`, `crates/api`, `crates/mcp`, `openapi/`, `web/client`, `web/app` |
| Depends on | 5.3; its screens 8.7, 8.9 |

## Goal

Let a whole branch be set aside in one move. Any container with unfinished in-scope work (a group, or a deliverable or action with children) can be snoozed until a date or until a named node completes, and while that snooze holds every descendant leaves the next list and triage, naming the container's snooze as the reason. Today only actionable nodes can be snoozed, and groups are never actionable, so setting aside a branch means snoozing each of its frontier nodes one by one, and anything that reaches the frontier later is not covered.

## Governing

- PRD: B6 (containers, the subtree rule for targets, holding over the subtree, what clears it), D2 and D3 (`snoozed` derived through an ancestor), D8 (display state `snoozed`), D5 (stalled names a container's snooze), Core concepts (Snooze, Acting frontier), C9, C10, C11 (filters and actions), J3 (scenario).
- ARCHITECTURE: Engine > Read path: derive, Write path: apply; Web UI (Screens).
- PRACTICES: Bounds and assertions.

## Scope

In: accepting a snooze on a container; rejecting a target in the container's subtree or one that transitively depends on anything in it; deriving each descendant's `snoozed` from its own snooze and every ancestor's, with `snoozed_via` naming the container whose snooze holds it, beside the existing `snoozed` target (additive on the wire); clearing on a transition of the container itself only (for a group, skip and reopen); unsnooze. On screen, with 8.7 to 8.10: the Snooze popover's "set aside" choice (the node, then each ancestor container with unfinished in-scope work and its open count, nearest first; its node targets exclude the scope's subtree and anything depending on it); Snooze branch... in a container's overflow menu; the container card's chip "Snoozed until <date or node>"; descendants saying "via <container>" in their foot, not dimmed, since snoozed work still blocks and counts; the sentence "Snoozed through <container>" with Unsnooze <container>; the Next page's Snoozed fold grouped by the snooze that holds each node; the stalled diagnostic naming the container; bulk snooze in the Plan list; on a container holding its own snooze, the line above Start and Mark done: "Starting this lifts the snooze on <container> and its N open items". The model parts do not wait for the screens.

Out: any change to what a snooze does to actionability, blocking, or gravity (B6, D2: unchanged).

## Acceptance

- Behavior by id: B6 (accept on a group and on a deliverable with children; reject the subtree and dependency cases with their paths; a descendant completing does not clear it; skip and reopen of the group do; a node target completing lifts it), D3, D5 (an acting frontier emptied by one container snooze is stalled and names it, with unsnooze), C9 (the snoozed filter lists descendants snoozed through a container).
- Scenario over the vendor evaluation: snooze Testing until the decision meeting is reached; answering the partner decision brings partner-led nodes onto the frontier already snoozed; a descendant with its own later snooze stays hidden after the container's lifts; replay matches.
- Playwright: the inspector of a snoozed container shows the lift-snooze line beside Start, and starting it clears the snooze for every descendant.
- Planted bug: clear the container's snooze when a descendant transitions, and show the scenario fail.

## Decisions left to the implementer

- Whether a descendant with its own later snooze is listed once or under both snoozes in the Snoozed fold.

## Decision log

- `NodeDerived.snoozed_via` (and `NodeRow.snoozed_via`) name the nearest container whose snooze
  holds over an open, in-scope node. `snoozed` stays the one target that holds the node: its own
  when that holds, else the container's, so every existing reader keeps working and a node snoozed
  twice shows its own target beside the container.
- A container's snooze applies to each descendant that is open and in scope; finished, skipped and
  not-relevant descendants are not marked, so a snooze left on a finished container holds nothing.
- A group is snoozed only while it has an open, in-scope, non-group node beneath it (checked on the
  graph the patch produces); a deliverable or action with children keeps the actionable-node rule.
- The wait-cycle check gives a container's snooze one wait per node in its subtree, so a target
  inside the subtree or depending on anything in it is `snooze_cycle`. This rejects a deliverable
  snoozed until its own child, which the earlier node-only rule accepted; a stored snooze like that
  would now fail validation (none exists in the fixtures or the deployed data).
- Unsnooze on a node with no snooze of its own that sits under a snoozed container is judged on the
  graph the patch produces: refused with `snoozed_through_container` while the container's snooze
  still holds over it, otherwise "not snoozed". A bulk that unsnoozes the container too is judged by
  that final graph.
- Stalled names a container's snooze once as `StallCause::Snooze` on the container (no wire change),
  followed by its target as a gate, however many frontier nodes it holds.
- No store migration: snoozes are stored by node key regardless of kind, so existing databases load
  unchanged.
