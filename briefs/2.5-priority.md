## Goal

The priority formulas exactly as the PRD states them, each signal carrying its inputs: gravity with its contributors, leverage with its unblock set split by owner, rank with its terms, the global rank and the per-viewer recomputation, tie-breaking, and effort-adjusted ordering.

## Governing

- PRD: Core concepts (Weight, Gravity, Leverage, Rank); Priority (normative formulas and defaults); C6, C10 (the data they need); D3.
- ARCHITECTURE: Engine > Read path: derive, passes 6 and 7 (leverage for every node in the normalization set, in one pass); Terms (Derive inputs).
- PRACTICES: Explicit limits (weight); Back-of-the-envelope first.

## Scope

In: gravity over 2.2's graph with per-node undecided discount, count-once, not-relevant pruning, and terminal traversal; `max_child_gravity`; leverage for every node in the normalization set; rank with normalization, null slack, tie order, and `RankConstants` from the derive inputs; `rank_for(viewer)` leaving the global rank unchanged; effort-adjusted ordering.

Out: sort and filter surfaces (2.6, 5.3); heat display (5.2).

## Acceptance

- Behavior: every Priority formula clause has a scenario (undecided discount on own and downstream weight; a relevant node behind an undecided one counts in full; terminal traversal; count-once; owner factor including the unowned target; null slack; tie order; per-viewer recomputation); the illustrative example's "environment access has higher gravity than the plan"; a small gate with high leverage and low gravity; C6 and C10 data present.
- Properties: gravity never decreases upstream along a dependency between in-scope nodes; gravity is at least the node's own effective weight; leverage counts each target once; rank is in [0, 1]; two derives give identical order; weight sums fit the integer type at the limits.
- Planted bug: count a downstream node twice on a diamond and show the count-once scenario fail.
- Fixtures: the vendor evaluation's rank order at start and after the four up-front decisions, recorded in the fixture README and asserted.

## Decisions left to the implementer

- An exact representation that supports the configured undecided discount (fixed-point is expected).
- Float or fixed point for rank terms (float is fine: rank is never stored and ties break on exact values and keys).
