## Goal

The priority formulas exactly as the PRD states them, each signal carrying its inputs: gravity with its contributors, leverage with its unblock set split by owner, rank with its terms, the global rank and the per-viewer recomputation, tie-breaking, and effort-adjusted ordering.

## Governing

- PRD: Core concepts (Weight, Gravity, Leverage, Rank); Priority (normative formulas and defaults); C6, C10 (the data they need); D3.
- ARCHITECTURE: Engine > Read path: derive, passes 6 and 7 (leverage for every node in the normalization set, in one pass); Terms (Derive inputs).
- PRACTICES: Explicit limits (weight); Back-of-the-envelope first.

## Scope

In: gravity over 2.2's graph with per-node undecided discount, count-once, not-relevant pruning, and terminal traversal; `max_child_gravity`; leverage for every node in the normalization set; rank with normalization, null slack, tie order, and `RankConstants` from the derive inputs; `rank_for(viewer)` leaving the global rank unchanged; effort-adjusted ordering.

Out: sort and filter surfaces (2.6, 5.3); heat display (5.2).

## Acceptance

- Behavior: every Priority formula clause has a scenario (undecided discount on own and downstream weight; a relevant node behind an undecided one counts in full; terminal traversal; count-once; owner factor including the unowned target; null slack; tie order; per-viewer recomputation); the illustrative example's "environment access has higher gravity than the plan"; a small gate with high leverage and low gravity; C6 and C10 data present.
- Properties: gravity never decreases upstream along a dependency between in-scope nodes; gravity is at least the node's own effective weight; leverage counts each target once; rank is in [0, 1]; two derives give identical order; weight sums fit the integer type at the limits.
- Planted bug: count a downstream node twice on a diamond and show the count-once scenario fail.
- Fixtures: the vendor evaluation's rank order at start and after the four up-front decisions, recorded in the fixture README and asserted.

## Decisions left to the implementer

- An exact representation that supports the configured undecided discount (fixed-point is expected).
- Float or fixed point for rank terms (float is fine: rank is never stored and ties break on exact values and keys).

## Decision log

Appended by the implementer.

- Calls that shape the design are in `decisions/`: the discount and factor as rank constants with exact millionth scores; what counts toward gravity and what leverage completes; the owner factor over owner sets; the projection onto the schema's `Derived` landing here.
- Layout: `cairn_engine::derive::priority` holds pass 6 (`Priority`: per-node counted weight, downstream bit rows, gravity, `max_child_gravity`, owners, unblocked targets, leverage; `priority/leverage.rs` the simulations); `derive::rank` holds pass 7 (`Ranking`, `RankTerms`); `derive::project` holds `Derived::to_schema`. `Derived` gains `priority()`, `ranking()`, `rank_for(viewer)`, `by_effort(graph, keys)`, and `rank_constants()`; it is no longer `Eq` (rank terms are floats).
- Pass 5 keeps its topological order (`Blocking::order`), which pass 6 reads backward rather than ordering the instants again.
- `derive_at` takes the rank constants; the write path's derived stage passes the defaults, since no guard reads gravity, leverage, or rank (one derive function, at a few milliseconds of unused work per guarded patch at the limits).
- A zero urgency horizon is the limit of a shrinking one: urgency 1 from slack 0, late 1 below it, so no division by zero.
- Effort-adjusted ordering compares gravity per estimated day exactly (cross-multiplied integers); nodes with no or a zero estimate (decisions and milestones have none) follow, and ties fall back to the global rank order. It sorts any list of keys, so 2.6 can apply it to the next list or C9's list.
- `max_child_gravity` is the largest gravity among a container's in-scope children (0 when none is in scope), none for a node without children.
- Gravity's contributors list only nodes that count something; `Priority::downstream` lists the whole set, terminal nodes included, for C7's trace.
- Generated journeys now carry weights from 0 to 5 on three quarters of their nodes (from the recorded-finish seed's spare bits, so other generated values are unchanged).
- J3 entries added: the vendor evaluation's ranked frontier at creation, after the up-front decisions, and after kickoff (fixtures/README.md states the table, read at the matrix's clock); "prioritize for me" on the product launch with its feature work given to a writer (the writer's ranking puts the beta start before the launch, the shared one the reverse, unchanged by the per-viewer ranking).
- Cost at the limits (measured, `date_limits` with every weight at `weight_max`): 1,749,097 operations as stored and 1,790,826 with every node open, against a budget of 1,843,747 (32 words x 54,659 pruned edges for the union, plus each instant, pruned edge, and kept-work entry once); the largest gravity is 2,000,000 weights, within `Score::MAX` (`tests/cost_priority.rs`, `briefs/proof/2.5/README.md`).
- Planted bug: gravity summed along every path instead of over the count-once downstream set fails the diamond scenario (`thread 'a_diamond_counts_its_bottom_once' panicked at crates/engine/tests/priority.rs:105:5`, left 23.0, right 13.0, 1 of 12 failing); restored, all pass (`briefs/proof/2.5/README.md`, generated by `prove.sh`).
- Review round 1 (`gpt-6.1-sol`), two findings, both valid, fixed with regression tests: (P2) leverage's simulations did not cascade through an `auto_reach` milestone due today or earlier, which pass 5 reads as reached once unblocked, so a gate in front of one was not credited for what the milestone releases; it now completes like a group (`leverage_cascades_through_a_due_auto_reach_milestone`, and a property against derive with `auto_reach` kept, read late enough that dated milestones are due), with [`decisions/2026-10-06-what-counts-toward-gravity-and-what-leverage-completes.md`](../decisions/2026-10-06-what-counts-toward-gravity-and-what-leverage-completes.md) and the cost test's budget updated (333 overdue, gated milestones on the limits graph read on 2026-12-01 add 414 operations). (P3) the discount and factor accepted values near the grid or just out of range (`-0.000001` as 0, `0.5000009` as 0.5); parsing now allows float error only and checks the value's own sign.
- Review round 2 (`gpt-6.1-sol`), two findings. (P2, declined and recorded in [`decisions/2026-10-06-what-counts-toward-gravity-and-what-leverage-completes.md`](../decisions/2026-10-06-what-counts-toward-gravity-and-what-leverage-completes.md)) leverage holds dates fixed, so an `auto_reach` milestone that completing a node would newly bring due through the date network (its first effective date bounded by that node's actual) is unblocked but not completed: re-solving dates per hypothetical completion would be up to 2,000 date solves per derive at the limits. (P3, valid, fixed with a regression check in the schema's tests) rank coefficients each finite but summing past any float made derive panic on an infinite rank; `RankConstants` now rejects coefficients whose sum is not finite when it is read.
