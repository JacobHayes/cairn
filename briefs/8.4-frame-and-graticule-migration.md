# 8.4: Frame and Graticule migration

| | |
|---|---|
| Id | 8.4 |
| Crates or packages | `web/app`, `design/`, `mise-tasks/` |
| Depends on | 5.8 |

## Goal

Move the web app onto DESIGN.md and give it a frame that never traps a scroll. Today the app has its own styles beside Graticule's, panels guess their heights, and the detail panel sits sticky inside a scrolling page, so its last section is cut off until the page itself scrolls. The frame fills the viewport with a rail, a workspace, an inspector column, and a status strip, each region scrolling once, and every screen is reskinned to DESIGN's tokens and components, calm by default (DESIGN, Approachable by default).

## Governing

- DESIGN: Approachable by default; Layout (Shell, One scroller per region, Inspector, Responsive); Components (Table, Status, Keyboard hints, Toast, Status strip, Segmented control, Popover and menu, Bottom sheet, Selection bar); Typography; Dark theme; Accessibility.
- PRD: Non-functional (Small-team scale); H6 (the strip will carry liveness).
- ARCHITECTURE: Web UI; Build, run, deploy (assets embedded).
- PRACTICES: The validation ladder; Growing the ladder.

## Scope

In:
- `design/tokens.css` and `design/base.css` imported through a Vite alias, with no copies; the fonts inlined, hashed, and served with the long cache header. `design/*.css` updated to DESIGN as amended (status dot variants and shapes, the new components, dark-mode inspector, roomier rows, sentence-case controls).
- The frame: a `100dvh` grid of rail, workspace, inspector column, and a 28px graphite status strip; `body` never scrolls at 720px and wider. The only scrolling elements are the rail, the workspace body, the inspector body, a sheet body, a popover body, and the key-sheet modal body. The sticky `100vh` panels, guessed canvas heights, `max-height: 70vh` table wraps, and the detail panel's scroll workaround are removed.
- The rail: Mine, Journeys, Library, Entities, up to five recent journeys (each reopening its last page), and You (identities, tokens, a System / Light / Dark theme control, and licenses crediting xyflow, ELK, and the fonts). A short inline script sets the theme before first paint; React Flow gets the resolved theme.
- The inspector column: 400px, collapsed and restored with `]`; the assistant becomes a tab of its head beside the inspector, its transcript the inspector body and its composer pinned below, replacing the floating panel. In dark mode the column, sheet, and strip sit on the ground colour.
- The strip shows only the existing live status at its right end (8.5 replaces it with the sync chip) and "? Keys"; `?` opens the key sheet modal, and a small key registry lets each page bind its keys (never while a field has focus). Esc closes a popover, then clears the selection, then closes a sheet.
- The kit reskin and class renames where app classes clash with `base.css` (buttons, fields, badges, panels, tables, selects, callouts, the shell; the canvas's `.card*` classes become `.node*`), each CSS file migrated through temporary token aliases removed by the end. Buttons, menu items, toolbar text, and tabs in sentence case; list rows about 64px.
- Responsive (may follow in its own change): from 720 to 1099px the inspector is a bottom sheet (drag handle, snap to 40dvh or 85dvh, Esc closes); below 720px one scrolling column, and a graph or timeline renders as an inert fitted preview (no pan, zoom, or scroll capture) with one "Open map" button opening a full-screen layer where gestures belong to the canvas, closed by Esc or its close button, with `map=1` in the URL so Back closes it.
- Guard rails: a `lint:css` rung failing on `overflow(-y): auto|scroll` outside the six scrollers, `max-height` in `vh`, `position: sticky` outside table headers, the timeline axis, and the narrow strip, raw hex, a radius over 4px, and `100vh`; a Playwright check on one screen per width (a canvas at 1440x768, the summary at 1024x700, the sheet over the map at 390x844), in one theme, that scrolls the inspector to the end and asserts History is inside the viewport and the document's scroll position is 0 (the inspector is one component and the CSS lint holds each region to one scroller, so the screens are not multiplied by widths); a 390x844 touch check that a swipe over the map preview scrolls the page and leaves the canvas transform alone.

Out: inspector drag-resize and print styles (later, additive); the sync chip (8.5); the journey toolbar, tabs, and switcher (8.6); per-screen layouts beyond the reskin (8.6 to 8.11).

## Acceptance

- Every existing end-to-end spec passes with its test ids untouched.
- The History check passes at each of the three widths and fails on the old CSS; at 390x844 with touch, a swipe over the map preview scrolls the page and does not pan.
- `lint:css` runs in `mise run check:fast` and fails on a planted `overflow-y: auto` (the planted bug).
- A visual pass in light and dark at 390, 1024, and 1440px.
- Proof: before-and-after screenshots of the canvas, a list, and the inspector, and the dark theme.
