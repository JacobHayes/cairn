# 6.2: Durability testbed

| | |
|---|---|
| Id | 6.2 |
| Crates or packages | `testbeds/durability` |
| Depends on | 4.1 |

## Goal

Simulation track: exploratory and non-blocking (README, Order). The service on the real Turso store under filesystem faults (EIO, short writes, crash-restart with torn writes), checking that after every restart state equals the replayed log and no acknowledged commit is missing.

## Governing

- PRD: J2, J3, A17.
- PRACTICES: Simulation with `patina` (the `durability` paragraph; the honesty rules).
- ARCHITECTURE: Storage > Backends (Turso).

## Scope

In: a `main` running the service on the Turso store in a simulated filesystem, driving commits, crashing and restarting on a seed-chosen schedule, reloading every domain and replaying its events through the engine; `always!` for the invariants; `sometimes!` that a crash landed between state rows and events and that a torn write was repaired on recovery; `mise run sim` and `cargo-patina` if 1.3 has not landed; a README.

Out: network faults (6.1).

## Acceptance

- A campaign passes with every `sometimes!` fired.
- Planted bug: write events outside the state transaction, get a failing seed that minimizes to the crash point, and rewrite it as a conformance case in 3.1's suite.

## Decisions left to the implementer

- Crash schedule, fault rates, whether to reuse 6.1's actors.

## Decision log

Appended by the implementer.

- 2026-10-07. Crash schedule: an explicit sweep (every 11th write and sync, block and byte tearing, two seeds), because a campaign draws no crash ([`decisions/2026-10-07-patina-a-campaign-draws-no-crash-and-no-crash-leaves-part.md`](../decisions/2026-10-07-patina-a-campaign-draws-no-crash-and-no-crash-leaves-part.md)); fault rates: the campaign's bands at 30 per mille of patina's defaults plus swarm, the crash-under-errors sweep at 5 per mille. 6.1's actors are not reused: one sequential client with a durable ledger is what a crash-restart can be judged by.
- Invariants are `violation` verdicts with exit 1, not `always!` (6.1's patina entry: an abort leaves no trace, and a crash-restart run's violation must replay).
- The planted bug is the client's (`--bug ack-before-commit`): fault-free it passes; a crash after the 40th sync fails `durability-acknowledged-commit-present` (`step 6 was acknowledged at revision 1 and has no events`). The brief's store plant (events outside the state transaction) was not applied: the session's permission policy refused the temporary edit to `crates/store-turso/src/commit.rs`. That plant's crash is already 3.1's `a_crash_mid_commit_keeps_every_earlier_commit_and_nothing_of_its_own`; the testbed's crash between state rows and events (leg 5) is the same situation under the real filesystem.
- The finding the sweep made is the brief's conformance case instead: `crates/store-turso/tests/conformance/log_sync.rs`, ignored until the fix, behind a new test seam `TursoStore::open_with_io` (feature `io-seam`, `turso_core` named only there). Edits outside `testbeds/durability`: that seam and test, `races.rs`'s `add_node` made `pub`, the root `turso_core` workspace dependency.
- A failed open is not retried in-process (Turso panics on it); an erroring disk that keeps the store from opening is `outcome=unavailable`, a pass. Exactly one Turso panic at open (`failed to get file size`) is caught and counted the same; any other panic unwinds.
- The spike (`testbeds/spike`) is left in place: 6.1 inherits its network gap legs.
