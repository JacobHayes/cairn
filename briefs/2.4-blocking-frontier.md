## Goal

Decide what can be acted on, and close the write path. From 2.2's graph and effective skip and 2.3's bounds, derive auto-reach, `deps_done`, `blocked`, `actionable`, the frontier, `snoozed`, the acting frontier, `stalled` with what it waits on, `needs_breakdown`, and `stale`; add `deps_done` and relevance on completing transitions to the validation pipeline; provide `consequences`.

## Governing

- PRD: Core concepts (Frontier through Guard); Containment (what it blocks); Gating (blocked, the frontier); A17 (guards within an ordered patch); B6; D1, D1a (what satisfies a dependency); D2, D4, D5, D7; F1 (auto-reach); Invariants (snooze wait cycles).
- ARCHITECTURE: Engine > Read path: derive, pass 5 and `stale` in pass 7; Write path: apply.
- PRACTICES: Back-of-the-envelope first; Code conventions (deterministic by construction).

## Scope

In: auto-reach evaluated in dependency order inside the blocking pass; the blocking pass over the pruned graph; snooze holding derived from current state; the snooze wait-cycle validation stage; the acting frontier and `stalled` with its waiting list as data; `stale` against the transition each node used and the failures its bypass recorded; the derived-guard stage, which derives the final candidate once and names the mutation that caused a failure; `consequences(before, after)`.

Out: the stored half of snooze clearing (2.1); priority (2.5); surfaces (2.6, 5.2, 5.3).

## Acceptance

- Behavior by id: Gating (each decision placement yields the stated frontier; an undecided dependency blocks like an open one), Containment (the plan reaches the frontier only after its child actions; an edge into a parent waits for the parent; an empty group waits for its opening gate), B6 (date and node snoozes; lifts and holds again; cleared by a transition and the clearing replays; self-wait and wait cycles rejected), D1a (kept work holds a skipped container's dependents), D2, D4 (each guard and bypass; `stale` after an answer change and after an inserted dependency; a skipped node is never stale for what its skip did not require), D5 (`stalled` names a gate, a snooze, and an `auto_reach` date), F1 (auto-reach flips with the date, reverses when a dependency reopens, chains through two milestones; a pending `auto_reach` milestone pinned yesterday with satisfied dependencies reads as reached).
- The 2.2 reference test is extended: blocking over the entry chain equals blocking over the plain expansion.
- Multi-mutation patches: bulk completion of a chain in any order is accepted; completing a node and adding an unfinished dependency to it in one patch is rejected naming both mutations, and as two patches leaves it `stale`.
- D7: an inserted dependency reports the completion it made stale; a late actual reports its shortfall; a patch across midnight reports nothing the date alone caused.
- Planted bug: leave snoozed nodes in the acting frontier and show the D5 scenario fail.
- Fixtures: the vendor evaluation yields the frontier the PRD's illustration describes; Setup is not actionable until kickoff.
