| | |

## Goal

Three read-mostly screens over 2.6's projections: the decision view, the timeline with overdue and shortfall marked and the `final` milestone, if any, as the end anchor, and the status summary for observers.

## Governing

- PRD: C12, C13, C18, F6 and F7 (presentation on the timeline); Users (Observers).
- ARCHITECTURE: Web UI (Screens).
- PRACTICES: Code conventions (TypeScript).

## Scope

In: the three screens, each opening 5.1's panel on click; the summary as a printable page.

Out: editing beyond opening detail; the markdown export (Later).

## Acceptance

- Behavior by id: C12, C13 (with and without a `final` milestone), C18, F6 and F7 presentation.
- Playwright: the partner decision gates the partner-led subset; the timeline anchors on the decision meeting and marks a shortfall after the fixture's late-actual scenario; summary counts match the fixture README.
- Review fixes: printing resets every color token (more specific than the theme's), so a dark tab prints black on white, muted text and badges included; the tabs carry the canvas's query; `nodeOf` looks nodes up in a key index built once per document's node list (decision rows at the node limit, 20 decisions each affecting 1,000 nodes: 93 ms to 2 ms).
- Planted bug: anchor the timeline on the latest pin instead of the `final` milestone and show the C13 test fail.

## Decisions left to the implementer

- Timeline drawing (plain SVG is expected), axis granularity.

## Decision log

Appended by the implementer.

- Addresses: each screen sits at the journey's address plus its segment (`/journeys/<id>/decisions`, `/timeline`, `/summary`) and opens node detail at that address plus `/nodes/<key>`. The panel's node links and its close now keep the screen they are on (`screenPath` in `detail/parts.tsx`), so 5.1's panel serves every screen unchanged otherwise; the canvas keeps its own query. The three screens join the acting surfaces' journey tabs (`screens/JourneyNav.tsx`); among the canvas and these three, a tab keeps an open node open and carries the address's query, so the canvas's settings survive a visit (these screens read no query). The acting surfaces keep their own settings in their query and are linked fresh. The three screens share a frame (`screens/JourneyScreen.tsx`): the journey read and derived in the tab, its name and the day it was derived for, the tabs, and the panel.
- Decision view: the engine's projection drawn with 5.2's cards and lines (laid out under its own layout view, `decision-view`), above a table of each decision, its state, its answer in effect, its owners, and what the answer affects: each node whose relevance it decides with that node's relevance now, the milestone it pins with its date, the role it fills, and what it waits on that no decision stands for. No fixture has a decision gated by another, and ELK stacks unconnected cards in one column, so with no gating edge the cards are set in tree order in a grid of up to four columns (`decisionPlacement`); with any edge ELK's layered layout stands. The canvas grows with its cards within 240 to 720 px. A card's hidden-prerequisites marker opens that decision's trace on the journey canvas.
- Timeline drawing: HTML rows, each a label beside a plain SVG track; every track and the axis share one width and place dates as percentages of it, so today (dashed, accent) and the end anchor (solid) run down every row as one line, and a row's "Why" opens below it without breaking the alignment. Shapes: a diamond for a milestone, a circle for anything else; an actual solid, a pin filled with the accent, a derived due date hollow; overdue or short outlined in the bad color, with a badge saying how many days short. What lies after the end anchor is shaded and still drawn. On a narrow screen the label sits above its track and every other tick label is dropped.
- Axis granularity: the span is today and every date with two days of margin; ticks are the finest of days, weeks (Mondays), months, quarters, or years (several years per tick on a very long span) that keeps them to twelve.
- F7 on the timeline: each row tells actual, pin, and derived due apart in its label and its shape; its "Why" shows node detail's dates section for the node (effective date, earliest and latest start, due, slack, each with its chain and what to edit, and a shortfall with its moves), read-only: resolving is node detail's.
- Status summary: key figures (in scope, remaining, overdue, short, stale, open decisions), then upcoming milestones (date, where it comes from, owner), open decisions in rank order with owners, overdue nodes with how many days late, short nodes with how many days, stale nodes with why, and counts by state in lifecycle order (`derived` shown as groups). Printing (`@media print` in `screens/screens.css`) keeps only the screen, in black on white, with no shell, tabs, buttons, panel, notices, or selection outline; a Print button calls the browser's print.
- The browser tests and the proof read the in-browser host with the page's clock held at noon UTC on 2026-10-06 (Playwright's clock), the day the fixture README's tables are read on, so overdue and due-soon do not move with the calendar; the server-host tests compare the page with the server's projection on whatever day it is.
- The fixture README gains a status summary line per fixture; the summary's browser tests rebuild each line from the page and require it in the README.
- Earlier work changed: node detail's links and close follow the screen (`detail/parts.tsx`, `detail/NodeDetail.tsx`); `roleTitle` is exported from `detail/sections.tsx`; the journey tabs (`screens/JourneyNav.tsx`) gain the three screens, the open node, and the canvas's query.
- Passing checks: `mise run check` (every rung), and `briefs/proof/5.4/prove.sh`.
- Planted bug: the timeline anchored on the latest pin instead of the `final` milestone (`endAnchor` in `timeline/model.ts`), caught by C13's unit tests with and without a final milestone: `FAIL src/timeline/model.test.ts > C13: the end anchor > is the final milestone at its date, even with a pin after it`.
