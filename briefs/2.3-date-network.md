# 2.3: Date network

|---|---|

## Goal

The PRD's one constraint model: build the network of instants and difference constraints, fix pins, detect contradictory chains in the plan as the invariant that rejects patches, and solve the execution layer, where actuals are facts and today holds unfinished work back, into bounds, `overdue`, and `shortfall`, each with its chain.

## Governing

- PRD: Core concepts (Pin through Shortfall); A8, A9; F1 to F7; Invariants (the date line); Non-functional: Small-team scale.
- ARCHITECTURE: Engine > Date network; Read path (the start-and-finish graph the network is built over); Assumptions (today is an input).
- PRACTICES: Fixed-width integers; Back-of-the-envelope first; Explicit limits (offsets, estimates, chains per rejection).

## Scope

In: a translation table in the module doc, written first and tested against: for every kind its instants (PRD F2), and for every rule, dependency, estimate, containment link, and stage bound the constraints it becomes and whether each holds a date back or caps it, including what a date answer and a pin contribute; network construction from 2.2's graph; the plan check as a validation stage and in construction validation, listing chains up to the limit with sources, pins, and shortfall days; the execution layer's bounds, `due`, `latest_start`, `slack`, effective dates, `overdue`, and `shortfall` with chains and the F5 resolution moves as data; a cost test at the limits counted in operations; the derive benchmark at the limits (elapsed time and peak memory, native), reported in the proof as a table.

Out: slack's use in rank (2.5); timeline and detail rendering (2.6, 5.1, 5.4).

## Acceptance

- Behavior by id: A8 (before and after, offset default, several sources, `created_at`, `not_before`), F1, F2 (every constraint source in at least one scenario), F3 (the PRD's "why is this due Tuesday?" chain), F4 (`closes: false`; a stage with neither end has null latest bounds and earliest bounds from its unfinished contents; started work whose latest start has passed is not a shortfall while its finish still fits), F5 (two rules placing a milestone before each other are rejected with no pin; a rejection past the chain limit says so; the illustrative example's later report pin is rejected with its chain; every resolution move accepted), F6 (a late actual never rejects or blocks an unrelated patch; a deadline relative to a reached milestone follows its actual), F7 (data side).
- Cycles: two milestones each placed no earlier than a day before the other are accepted (a negative cycle) and get bounds; the same with offsets that close a positive loop are rejected with the chain. Two-way propagation: "report due 14 days before the meeting" pushes an unpinned meeting's earliest date. Two actuals that violate a rule between them produce a shortfall, not a dropped constraint. Today seeds only unfinished decisions, deliverables, and actions: a pending milestone pinned yesterday whose dependencies are done has no shortfall, and one whose dependency is still open does. Two pins with a chain between them that needs more days than they allow are rejected. The 2.2 reference test is extended: date bounds over the entry chain equal those over the plain expansion. Pins and actuals are bounds on their own instants: two unrelated pinned chains stay separate components.
- Properties: no accepted graph has a positive cycle in its plan layer; the execution layer yields bounds for any actuals on an accepted plan; removing a constraint never tightens a bound; a chain always ends at a pin, an actual, or today.
- Planted bug: drop the containment constraint and show the "child finishes before the parent's own work" scenario fail.
- Fixtures: the vendor evaluation's pinned decision meeting yields the due dates its README states; the product launch exercises several rules on one milestone.

## Decisions left to the implementer

- Solver details within ARCHITECTURE's algorithm choice, justified against the cost test.
- Chain representation (constraint ids resolved to sources at explanation time is expected).

## Decision log

Appended by the implementer.

- Layout: `cairn_engine::derive::dates` holds pass 4: `network` (instant slots and constraints; the translation table is its parent module's doc and the library's unit tests check it row by row), `solve` (Tarjan's components in topological order, queue-based Bellman-Ford inside cyclic ones, one routine for both directions with latest bounds as negated longest paths, an operation counter), `plan` (the plan check), `execution` (earliest and latest passes, shortfall), and `explain` (chains as shown, entry hops folded, resolution moves). Public: `Dates` (on `Derived`, raw bounds by key and `Dates::node(graph, key)` for `NodeDates` with chains), `check_plan`/`PlanCheck`. Calls that shape the design are in `decisions/` (what the plan layer holds; `Graph::new` takes the deployment; schema additions; how the execution layer reads facts, slack, and overdue; derive takes `created_on`).
- Instants: 2.2's four per node (start, finish, two entries) plus `created_at` and one answer slot per node, `5n + 1` slots; a decision's or milestone's start is mapped onto its finish, so it is one instant and its own work edge disappears. A zero-or-negative loop on one slot is dropped at build; a positive one is kept, so a rule on a milestone against itself is a contradiction.
- Chains are not stored: each slot keeps the constraint that last raised it, and a chain is walked back on demand (at most one step per slot, asserted). Entry hops are folded into the one `Dependency` they carry, from the requirement's finish to the waiting node, named `Inherited`, `Condition`, or `StageOpening` by the ancestor whose entry it was.
- Solver details, against the cost test: inside a cyclic component, slots that changed are relaxed in rounds from a queue; past as many rounds as the component has slots, each further round looks for a cycle among the predecessors (a positive cycle shows there once values pass every simple path), asserted to appear within twice the rounds. The plan check sets aside the cycle's widest constraint after each contradictory chain and splits the component again, so further searches find other conflicts, stopping one past `chain_count_per_rejection_max`.
- `overdue` is computed here, next to the bounds it needs (coordinator ruling), though ARCHITECTURE's pass list puts it in pass 7; 2.5's rank and later projections read it from `Dates`.
- Fixture change: the product launch's late code freeze is now recorded on 2026-11-04 (step 4's clock moved with it). As written, 2026-11-02 was exactly the latest the shifted launch pin allows, so the scenario's "later than the chain allows" held only before the shift; the matrix's launch entry asserts the new date and the 2-day shortfall. `fixtures/README.md` states the vendor evaluation's due dates and the launch's shortfall.
- The generator (`testing::generated`) gives nodes estimates, rules from any milestone or `created_at`, stage closes, pins, and recorded dates, and drops the dates of nodes on any contradictory chain until the plan holds; `limit_seeds` and `date_limits` build the limits graphs the 2.2 linearity test, the cost test, and the benchmark share.
- Rung 3 now runs `tests/cost_*.rs` (`mod cost`) as suites beside the property tests, and rung 2 skips them by name.
- Cost at the limits (measured, `date_limits`: 2,000 nodes, both rules with 64 sources on every node, 10,001 slots, 310,202 constraints): the plan check about 1.67 million operations, derive's two passes about 1.60 million, against a budget of 16 operations per constraint per solve; natively, a plan check and a whole derive each take about 115 ms and peak resident memory is about 74 MiB (`briefs/proof/2.3/README.md`). The stated worst case, every slot in one component needing a round per slot, is about 3.7 x 10^9 relaxations; the generated limits graph needs a few rounds.
- Planted bug: leaving the containment constraint out of the network fails `a_child_finishes_before_its_parents_own_work` (`thread 'a_child_finishes_before_its_parents_own_work' panicked at crates/engine/tests/dates.rs:178:5`, 3 of 13 failing); restored, all pass (`briefs/proof/2.3/README.md`, generated by `prove.sh`).
- Review round 1 (`gpt-6.1-sol`), three findings, all valid, fixed in one change with a regression test each: a chain's end offered pin moves even where today or an actual, not the pin, held that end (a decision pinned yesterday offered moving its pin earlier, which widened the gap); a chain whose ends one answer fixes (a date decision and the milestone it feeds) offered re-answering it, which moves both ends; and an entity merge rejected for a journey it breaks dropped the contradictory chains of that journey's plan. End moves now come from the seed that actually holds each end, are left out when today would still hold it, and are dropped when one answer holds both; the merge's violation carries the chains.
- Review round 2 (`gpt-6.1-sol`), one finding, valid, fixed with a regression test (`a_fact_too_early_for_created_at_or_an_answer_is_short`): a fact earlier than a rule from `created_at` or a date answer requires showed on no node, since such a violation shows at its upstream end and those sources are no node's instant. A fixed instant is now also short when a constraint from `created_at` or an answer holds it later than its fact, with that one constraint as its chain.
- Review round 3 (`gpt-6.1-sol`), two findings, both valid, fixed with a regression test each (unreviewed: three rounds is the limit): an end's pin was offered as a move where today, carried through the work's start, or another chain would still hold that end once the pin moved (a pinned three-day action whose finish today holds back offered moving and clearing its pin, which left most of the gap); and a move past the last date there is was offered, silently clamped. Each chain now carries what else holds its start back and caps its end, a pin or answer moved past those (or past the dates there are) is not offered, and dates are moved with checked arithmetic.
