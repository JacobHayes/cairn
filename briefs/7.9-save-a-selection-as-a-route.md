## Goal

Let someone who built a good sub-process inside one route or journey lift it out as a route of its own. Saving drafts a proposal for a new route the way save as route drafts one: the selection keeps its keys, top-most nodes stay roots (several are fine: a route may have many), and nothing that reaches outside the selection is carried; each cut is listed with "add it to the selection" as its remedy.

## Governing

- PRD: B15, B8 (the review it reuses), C14 and C19 (save a selection as a route), I2, I6, J1, J3.
- ARCHITECTURE: Engine > Save-as-route; Model (Proposal).
- DESIGN: Components (Multi-select).

## Scope

In:

- `save_selection_as_route(records, source, selection, route, name)`: `create_route`, `open_draft`, the structure as ordinary mutations, and review items; the `selection_saved_as_route` event.
- The boundary as an informational `Boundary` review item per cut: a crossing edge dropped; an outside condition cleared; an outside date-rule source dropped (the rule cleared when none remain); an outside stage bound or fed milestone cleared; an outside answer placeholder replaced by the decision's title; an inherited participation from outside written onto each top-most node; `final` and `default_owner` not carried. B8's participation mapping (no default owner) and exclusions apply, and no state is carried.
- API `POST .../save-selection-as-route`; MCP `save_selection_as_route`; a line in `instructions/SKILL.md`.
- Screens: Save a selection as a route acts on the existing selection (the inspector's actions for a journey, and a route draft's selection); a small form (name, id), then review in the new route's frame with Dropped and Cleared items naming what to add to the selection. Applying opens the draft.

Out: saving into an existing route as its next draft (PRD Later).

## Acceptance

- B15: a crossing edge is dropped and listed; an outside condition is cleared and listed; several top-most nodes stay roots.
- J3: the scenario "save-selection-as-route round trip" replays field by field.
- Playwright: select three nodes in a journey, save them as a route, see the Dropped edge in review, apply, and publish the draft.
- A real bug its tests catch: a crossing edge kept, so the publish is rejected for a dangling reference.
