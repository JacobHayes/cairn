# 8.5: Sync chip

| | |
|---|---|
| Id | 8.5 |
| Crates or packages | `web/app` |
| Depends on | 8.4 |

## Goal

Replace the save toast with one quiet status at the right end of the strip. Today every save raises a toast that has to be closed and says "Nothing became stale, short, overdue, or stalled", while lag, skew, and a dropped stream each have their own banner. One button tells, at a glance, whether what you see is current and whether your changes landed: green when nothing needs doing, steel while working, amber when it is safe to wait, red when your change is not landing until you act. Hover says it in a sentence; a click shows the detail and this tab's recent saves.

## Governing

- PRD: D7 (consequences at the moment of the edit; warnings only), H5 (stale-revision rejections and safe retry), H6 (views stay current), I5 (direct writes reported).
- ARCHITECTURE: Web UI (Version skew, Data flow, Drafts survive a reload).
- DESIGN: Components (Status strip and sync chip, Popover and menu, Toast); Approachable by default; Accessibility.
- PRACTICES: Code conventions.

## Scope

In:
- A sync status store (`data/sync.ts`) merging the stream status, the announced revision against the revision the view shows, version skew, an in-flight write count (around `submit` in both write paths), unresolved rejections, and the browser's online and offline events.
- The states, highest first: CONFLICT (red, `!` for the dot: an unresolved stale-revision rejection) > NOT SAVED (red: a rejected write, or skew holding an unsent edit) > NEW VERSION (amber: skew with nothing unsent; "Reload to keep saving") > OFFLINE (amber) > RECONNECTING (amber: the stream dropped; writes still go) > BEHIND (amber: a newer revision was announced and its refetch failed or has taken over 5s; click retries) > SAVING (steel ring: a write in flight over 300ms) > UPDATING (steel ring: an announced revision's refetch in flight over 300ms) > SAVED (green, for 2s after a write lands) > IN SYNC (green). The in-browser host shows DEMO with a hollow dot. BEHIND is not called stale, which names a node flag.
- Hover: one line per state ("In sync, live, revision 42, last change 12s ago by Ana"); the button's accessible name carries it.
- The popover (Esc closes): the state with live updates on or off; revision, engine version, and today, moved out of every page header; under "Needs you", each rejection and conflict with Go to it (opens the node, focuses the control) and Discard or Retry; under "Recent", this tab's last ten saves in memory, each with both halves of its consequences (warnings, and when 8.2 has landed, what it unlocked and moved in or out of scope), linked.
- A receipt component for the edited control (8.7 and 8.9 place it): "Saved", plus one sentence only for a warning consequence ("Kickoff is now overdue"); it goes at the next edit or when the node is left. A rejection shows at its control with the violation list.
- The toast store renamed from notices to activity (`data/notices.ts` becomes `data/activity.ts`), so "notice" means only the advisory finding (A20, 7.5); the toast kept only for non-sync confirmations ("Link copied"); the skew banner, the live-status widget, and the notices stack deleted.
- A polite live region announces "Saved" and a receipt's warning; NOT SAVED and CONFLICT are announced assertively.

Out (later, additive): Undo on receipts; an offline edit queue (OFFLINE is a state of the status only, with no kept-edit count and no retry queue; drafts still survive a reload as today).

## Acceptance

- Behavior by id: D7 (no informational consequence in the receipt; warnings shown), H5 (a conflict offers retry and discard at the control and in the popover), H6.
- Playwright screenshots of IN SYNC, SAVED, a receipt with a warning, SAVING (throttled), UPDATING (refetch throttled), BEHIND (refetch aborted after a revision event), RECONNECTING (stream aborted), OFFLINE (network aborted), NOT SAVED (a forced guard failure), CONFLICT (two tabs), NEW VERSION (a skewed engine version), and DEMO. Nothing on screen has a close button.
- Unit tests for the precedence and the colour rule.
- Planted bug: show OFFLINE in red, and show the colour-rule test fail.

## Decisions left to the implementer

- The Recent entry's wording per kind of write.
