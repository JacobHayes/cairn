## Decision log

Appended by the implementer.

- Card design (`canvas/NodeCard.tsx`, sized by `canvas/cards.ts`): a fixed width; a head line with kind and state, then title (up to two lines), owner, due with latest start and slack, a decision's prompt (up to two lines) and answer, derived flags and roll-up badges, a container's children line (most gravity, least slack, owners), the checklist of what rolls up (six items, then "and N more"), and the hidden-prerequisites marker. Every line has a fixed height, so the layout reads the size the card renders at; the rank badge, an overlay's mark, and the heat numbers hang on the card's edge and never move anything. A container's own card is its header, its children laid out below it.
- C5: the frontier and active work are outlined (started early is a badge); the viewer's own items get a lighter accent edge, since a viewer may own most of a journey and outlining it all would say nothing. The top five of the next list (the acting frontier by rank) carry numbered badges.
- C6 mapping: the due badge is red when overdue, amber when due within seven days, plain otherwise or once finished; latest start and slack beside it (dropped once finished); gravity maps to a border of 1 to 4 px against the largest gravity among open nodes; the heat toggle hangs "gravity N, leverage N" on each card. Nothing animates.
- ELK options (`canvas/layout.ts`): layered, left to right, `INCLUDE_CHILDREN` hierarchy handling, fixed seed, node and layer gaps of 28 and 56 px, semi-interactive crossing minimization when hints are given. The stability fraction is a quarter of the fixture's nodes, moved within their containers (DECISIONS.md, 5.2).
- Worker protocol: ELK's own. The layout worker is ELK's worker build loaded in a module worker, and the page talks to it through ELK's API (`elkjs/lib/elk-api.js`). ELK's bundled build in a worker of our own refused to start (it takes the worker's scope for itself), so a protocol of ours would have wrapped the same messages.
- What the canvas shows is in the address's query (`hide`, `in`, `notrelevant`, `undecided`, `heat`, `trace`), so links keep it: node detail's links and its close keep the query.
- C4 "expand in place" is the canvas itself with every kind shown (containers drawn with their children inside); "sub-canvas" is drilling in. A deliverable with actions shows them as nodes inside it, or, with actions hidden, as its checklist.
- The trace is a `CanvasOverlay`: marks by card (a card stands for itself and what rolls up into it), the lines it lights, a dimmed rest, and what it reaches off this canvas listed in the trace bar. Proposal review (5.7) draws its diff with the same type.
- A card is opened by clicking anywhere on it (React Flow's node click) or its title, which is a button for the keyboard; the drill and marker buttons stop the click.
- React Flow's attribution stays visible (its license asks it be kept unless subscribed).
- Earlier work changed: the journey page is the canvas (5.1's node list is gone, with its `.split` and `.row-selected` styles); the title editor (4.6's write path demonstration) moved into node detail as "Rename"; 4.6's and 5.1's browser tests and proof pictures open nodes from their cards and rename from the panel (`e2e/shell.ts`, `e2e/detail.ts`); `Host` gained `route` and `routeVersion`, `Deriver` gained `routeLevel` (fakes updated).
- A container's edge to its own child (a rolled-up requirement landing on a child's stand-in) is drawn from the container's side to the child's; ELK lays it out, React Flow draws it as a loop. Left as drawn: the engine's level draws it on purpose (DECISIONS.md, 2.6).
- The view fits the whole graph when what the canvas shows changes (a new flow mounts per view), never on a revision, so a live edit keeps the viewport.
- A card shows a node's effective state: skipped when an ancestor's skip covers it, reached when it reached itself on its date, so neither looks open or active; a group's due badge loses its urgency once the group is done. A canvas read is shown only for the request it answered (an earlier revision's answer stays until the next arrives), so a view's first layout is never hinted by another view's graph. Node detail is keyed by journey and node, so an editor's state never follows the panel to another node.
- Passing checks: `mise run check` (every rung), and `briefs/proof/5.2/prove.sh`.
- Planted bug: implicit edges drawn solid (`canvas/look.ts` drops the dotted dash), caught by C1's unit test: `FAIL src/canvas/model.test.ts > C1: cards and lines > the condition gate edge n_choose->n_option is drawn with the dash its kind gives, named by its source`.
