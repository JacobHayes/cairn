# The tour spotlights the action, then shows what changed

A person on the tour now sees the screen dim around the one thing to do, with the guidance beside it. When they do it, the dimming lifts and the guidance moves beside the change, so they watch Cairn work out the consequences on the live screen. Next dims the screen again on the following step. [A short video of the main flow](flow.webm) walks all five steps.

Known limits:

- On a phone the room above the callout holds one card at a readable size, so step 2 shows the decision and leaves the review card to the reveal.
- On a phone an open menu is taller than the room above the callout, so the callout covers its lower part; the control the step is about stays in the hole above it.
