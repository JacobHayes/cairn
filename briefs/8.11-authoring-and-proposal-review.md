# 8.11: Authoring and proposal review in the frame

| | |
|---|---|
| Id | 8.11 |
| Crates or packages | `web/app` |
| Depends on | 8.6, 8.7, 8.8 |

## Goal

Bring route authoring, journey structure edits, and proposal review into the same frame, graph, and inspector as everything else. Today the route canvas, the authoring panel, and proposal review each have their own layout and scroll containers, the node form shows every field at once, and review's canvas is a fixed-height box. A route draft opens like a journey's Plan page without state or dates; the inspector holds a node form whose sections open by kind and fold with their names; review shows the proposed graph with its changes tagged in words beside an item editor in the inspector.

## Scope

In:
- The route draft at `/routes/<id>/draft`: the Plan frame with Graph and List only (a route has no state or dates); a one-line header with the route name and "Draft (from v3)", Publish as the one primary, and an overflow menu with Discard draft, Export, and Import into draft. Cards show no status chip; their foot gives the date rule in words ("Due 14 days before Decision meeting"). The ladder, trace, and signals work as on a journey, with gravity and unblocks computed on the route and rank and slack left out; a route with no groups opens at the Decisions step.
- The draft card, the detail column's empty state on a route draft: violations (blocking publish) and a slot for notices (advisory; 7.5 fills it), and Publish and Discard draft.
- The node form in the inspector, on route drafts and in Edit structure: sections open, folded, or absent by kind, each folded section a row with its name. Basics (kind, title, id, description) open; Question (prompt, answer type, choices, help, fills role, feeds milestone) open for decisions and absent otherwise; Relevance, Dates (rules and stage bounds), Completion needs (requires artifact, requires note once 7.3 lands, placeholder), Weight and estimate, People, Guidance, and Provenance and local edits folded. Violations show at their field, previewed live as today, and in the draft card.
- Edit structure on a journey, from the journey menu: a band ("Editing structure: changes are patches to this journey" with Done), connecting and adding on the canvas, the Select mode (8.8) with Remove in the selection bar, and the node form in the inspector.
- Proposal review in the frame: a one-line header naming the proposal, its item count, and conflicts, Apply as the primary (disabled while conflicts are unresolved, saying why on hover) and Discard in the overflow menu; filter chips (all, conflicts, add, change, remove); Graph and List. On the graph, items are tagged Add (success), Change (warning), or Remove (error, struck, kept ghosted), always with the word, and cascaded removals are grouped under their cause. The inspector is the item editor: before and after, resolution choices, orphan keep or remove, participation mapping. The trace is off during review. Each region scrolls once.
- The Library's route detail: the versions table with the journeys on each and their upgrade availability.

Out: segment flows (insert, insertion upgrade, save as segment), which 7.7 builds on this frame; the notices themselves (7.5).

## Decision log

Appended by the implementer.

- A route has one page, `RouteCanvasPage`, in two frames: `/routes/<id>/draft` (authored) and
  `/routes/<id>?version=N` (read only). A draft opens like a version, at Stages or at
  Decisions with no groups, and adding a node moves the ladder to a step that draws it (decision
  record 2026-10-09-a-route-draft-opens-like-a-version-and-removal-is-one-node-at-a-time). The route ladder is a set of kinds per step (Stages draws groups
  and milestones), since the route level has no collapsed containers to ask the engine for.
- Draft violations are the engine's refusal of `publish_draft` previewed on the draft as it
  stands (with the route's published versions read first), so the draft card and the Publish
  button agree with what Publish would do. Notices come from `routeNotices` as before; the card's
  hollow chip and the draft card read the same list.
- Not built: the trace and the Signals lens on a route draft. They need a route trace and gravity
  from the host (a `routeTrace` beside `routeLevel`); the ladder, drill-in, and the List work.
- A proposal's node is picked at `/proposals/<id>/nodes/<key>`, so Esc and the inspector's close
  return to the proposal as they do on a journey. The proposal card (no node picked) lists what
  still needs a choice; each choice is made in the item editor of the node it is about, and
  confirming the review and what blocks Apply sit under the inspector whichever it shows.
- Edit structure keeps the node's detail above its form (the journey's inspector), and the band
  (`Editing structure: changes are patches to this journey`) replaces the Select band while it is
  on; Remove in its selection bar confirms node by node.
- The earlier `/routes/<id>/nodes/<key>` redirects to the draft's address with its query; a route
  whose only draft was discarded shows one line and Open a draft. Drilled into a stage with no step
  picked, a route reads at Work, since Stages draws only groups and milestones. On a tablet or
  phone graph, where the inspector has no column, the head says how many things the draft card
  would list and links to the List, which carries the card inline.
