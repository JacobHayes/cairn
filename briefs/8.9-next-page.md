## Goal

Make "what can be done now" calm and quick to act on. Today the next list splits into "for you" and "everyone else", each row carries a full breadcrumb and a row of buttons, and triage cards are their own component with their own layout. The Next page is one ranked list where each row says one thing, acts in one click, and opens the inspector for the rest; its cards projection renders the inspector's own component, one card at a time, for a pass and the decision walkthrough. Mine reuses the same rows across journeys.

## Governing

- PRD: C10 (one shared order, one reason per row, the primary action inline, only-mine), C11 (triage, pass, the walkthrough and its waiting view), C16 (cross-journey "mine"), D5 (stalled), B6 (snoozed), E1 (assign inline), H6 (liveness), I3 (the list matches the snapshot).
- ARCHITECTURE: Web UI (Screens; pass is client state).
- DESIGN: Approachable by default (show less, more air, plain words); Components (Table, Status, Dates).
- PRACTICES: Code conventions.

## Scope

In:
- List rows (about 64px): the display-state chip, the title, and one secondary fact in words: the largest reason the row ranks where it does ("Start today", "2 days late", "Due in 3 days", "Gates 6 nodes", "Unblocks 2 others' work") or a requirement ("Note required"); the owner shows as "You" or as an inline Assign when unowned, any other owner on hover and in the inspector; a conditional frontier item says "May not apply". Selecting a row (click, `j` and `k`) opens the inspector; on hover or selection the row shows its primary action at the right end (Done, or Done... when a note or link is required; Start for todo work; Mark reached; Decide, which focuses the inspector form), and 8.5's receipt after it.
- One list in the shared rank, no "for you" split; the Filter button's Mine hides the rest. When the viewer holds participations but none is on the frontier, one line answers them: "Nothing needs you now. Next for you: <their earliest-starting item>, after <what it waits on>".
- Folds, closed by default with their counts always shown: Needs a look (stale with its reason, snoozed and overdue, shortfall, each with its fix inline) and Snoozed (grouped by the snooze that holds it, once 7.4 lands). The stalled diagnostic replaces the list when the acting frontier is empty (D5), with Unsnooze and Confirm reached. Not-relevant rows never appear. Sort in the list header: rank, rank for me, due, start by, gravity, unblocks, slack, effort.
- Cards: the inspector's component (8.7) at a 560px maximum width with its sections folded, one at a time in rank order; the detail column holds the pass rail (up next, passed, done this pass, Start a new pass) instead; opening a linked node swaps the rail for that node's inspector with "Back to pass". Pass (key `p`) sits on the card frame's edge, apart from the node's own actions. With decisions on, the walkthrough: an intro line on a new journey's first visit, C11's waiting view when no decision is actionable, and at the end "No more decisions can be made now" with Continue with everything. End of pass: "Every card has been seen once" with Go round again and Start a new pass. Pass state stays per tab, as today.
- Mine: rows grouped by journey (alphabetical, each group in its journey's own rank, with its progress and Open), the "Next for you" line where nothing of the viewer's is actionable, and the inspector with its form, so a participant can work across journeys without opening each. The root address opens Mine when the viewer has anything actionable, and the journey index otherwise.

Out: Undo on receipts (later, additive); rows that collapse in place when someone else finishes them and a "2 new" bar holding back arriving rows (later, additive; until then rows update in place as today); unlocked-first pass order (7.1).

## Acceptance

- Behavior by id: C10 (one order matching the snapshot; one secondary fact per row; Mine filter), C11 (the walkthrough on start, pass, end of pass, the waiting view), C16 (Mine answers a decision without opening the journey), D5.
- Playwright: Done from a row's hover action with its receipt; a requires-note row's Done... completes with a note in one patch; in cards, a todo deliverable shows Mark done, Start, and the overflow menu, with Pass apart; screenshots of the list, the folds, the nothing-needs-you line, stalled, cards, the walkthrough, and Mine; a short video of a walkthrough pass.
- Planted bug: re-sort rows on hover, and show the order-stability test fail.

## Decisions left to the implementer

- Which reason wins when two rank terms are close.
- How the pass rail truncates a long queue.

## Decision log

- Which reason wins: a requirement ("Note required") first, then the largest weighted rank part;
  a date reason ("2 days late", "Start today", "Due in 3 days") wins when it is within 90% of the
  largest, since it is the one to act on today. When the list is sorted by one signal, that
  signal's reason leads.
- The pass rail names the first five of each group (up next, passed, done in this pass) and
  says how many more, so a long queue never pushes "Start a new pass" off the column.
- Pass sits at the top edge of the card frame with the card's place ("2 of 5"), not under the
  card: a long inspector would push it out of reach, and nothing sticky is allowed.
- A row's receipt is the shared one under the row's control; a row that leaves the list on Done
  takes its receipt with it, and the sync chip's Recent keeps the record.
- 8.7 has not landed, so a card is the existing node panel (`NodeDetailPanel`); it picks up the
  inspector's sections as 8.7 changes that component. Until then a card whose work needs a note
  or a link also shows the row's Done... flow in the panel's extra slot, so finishing it still
  adds them in one patch; a placeholder's Mark atomic is there too, and the panel offers no Complete
  until the placeholder is broken down or atomic.
