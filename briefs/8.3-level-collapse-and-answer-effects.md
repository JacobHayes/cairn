# 8.3: Level collapse and answer effects

| Depends on | 8.2 |

## Goal

Give the canvas's new semantic zoom, and the answer form, the engine projections they need. The level projection today takes shown kinds and one drilled-in container, and the browser hides not-relevant nodes after the fact, so their lines can be left dangling. The detail ladder (C2) needs the level to take collapsed containers and the display states shown, rolling hidden nodes up and re-targeting their edges under one rule. And a decision's form needs, for each choice, what answering it would bring in, drop, or leave for later, from the engine rather than guessed in the tab, with the same numbers available to agents.

## Governing

- PRD: C2 (the visibility rule: kind in the step's set, display state shown, no collapsed ancestor; roll-ups; re-targeting; the hidden-prerequisites marker), C1 (not relevant hidden by default), C3 (the whole graph at the All step), C8 (each choice's effect), D8 (pending nodes travel with conditional ones), Gating (three-valued evaluation), Non-functional: Small-team scale.
- ARCHITECTURE: Projections (`level`, `answer_effects`), Web UI (Data flow: on demand in the worker; agent parity), HTTP API, MCP endpoint, Generated artifacts.
- PRACTICES: Explicit limits; Testing > Engine: examples, properties, replay (cost tests at the limits).

## Scope

In:
- The level request gains `collapsed: Vec<NodeKey>` and the display states shown (default: everything but `not_relevant`). A collapsed container rolls its whole subtree into its card under C2's rules: edges re-target to the nearest visible stand-in, duplicates merge, and the hidden-prerequisites marker stays. Hidden not-relevant nodes roll up the same way and leave no orphan lines. The drilled-in container argument stays for compatibility. The tab-side relevance filter in `canvas/relevance.ts` is retired in favour of the request (the screens move over in 8.8).
- Each level node carries its progress roll-up (done of in-scope descendants) and, for a stand-in edge, how many edges it stands for and the strongest kind among them (requires, then condition, then stage opening).
- `answer_effects(decision)`: per choice of a boolean or choice decision, the nodes the answer would bring into scope, drop (settled not relevant), and leave decided later (pending on another decision, never counted as drops), and the milestone it pins and the role it fills; for multi choice, the effect of toggling each choice; for text, date, and entity answers only the pin and fill. A three-valued evaluation of relevance under each choice, not a full derive.
- Both through the wasm bindings and the worker messages, computed on demand for one decision per revision and cached; `answer_effects` also on API node detail and MCP `get_node` for decisions, and MCP's decisions-needed listing gains an `include_effects` flag.
- Cost tests: level with collapse within 16ms at 500 nodes and 60ms at 2,000; the trace for one selection within 8ms and 30ms.

Out: the ladder UI, expand in place, and fit (8.8); the preview line, which uses the existing draft-patch preview (8.7); `still_waiting` (8.2).

## Acceptance

- Behavior by id: C2 (unit tests: a collapsed stage re-targets incoming and outgoing edges, merges duplicates, keeps the hidden-prerequisites marker; hidden not-relevant nodes leave no orphan lines; a node under a collapsed ancestor is not visible whatever its kind), C3 (at All with every display state shown, the 2,000-node generated graph's level holds every node), C8.
- `answer_effects` on the vendor evaluation's partner decision: while it is open, "yes" brings in the partner-led subset and "no" drops it; once it is answered "yes", "yes" reports no change and "no" drops the subset. On the two-decision variant (8.1), the follow-up's gated deliverable is reported as decided later under the partner decision, never as a drop.
- `getNode` and MCP `get_node` return `answer_effects` for a decision; the decisions-needed tool returns them with `include_effects`.
- The cost budgets above, asserted at the limits.
- Planted bug: skip re-targeting for collapsed ancestors, and show the re-targeting test fail.
