## Goal

Make the Plan page's graph readable at a glance and drillable on demand. Today kind checkboxes are the only zoom, every card carries its prompt and description, edges squeeze a "condition" label into the gap so the arrow disappears, trace is a separate toggle, and a trackpad either zooms or traps the page. The graph opens at its stages with the current ones open, expands in place, traces on selection, routes edges where the layout left room for them, and tells edge kinds apart by their end marker. It can split into two changes: cards and edges first, then the ladder and lens.

## Governing

- PRD: C1 to C7 (cards, the ladder and the visibility rule, the whole graph, expand in place, "I am here", the signals lens, trace), C15 (stable layout), D8, Priority (peak gravity; rank tags), Non-functional: Small-team scale.
- ARCHITECTURE: Web UI (Canvas, Data flow).
- DESIGN: Charts (graphs); Approachable by default (graph cards, quieter chrome); Components (Status, Rank tag, Hanging tag, Selection bar, Dates).
- PRACTICES: Explicit limits; Testing (cost tests at the limits).

## Scope

In, cards and edges:
- Cards (`.node`, 240px, four corner ticks, paper fill): a head with the kind label at left and the display-state chip at top right; the title (15px/600, two lines at most); a body line only for a decided decision (its answer, and a mark when it has a rationale, whose hover shows the rationale's first paragraph as plain text) and a collapsed container (progress and at most one roll-up badge); one foot line with the most actionable date in words and the owner only when it is the viewer or missing. A rank tag hangs off the top-left of the top three on the acting frontier; frontier and active cards get an ink border; conditional cards are dashed with steel text and say on what they depend; not-relevant cards, when shown, are transparent and hollow; finished cards recede to the ramp. Card sizes stay computed before layout (`cards.ts` recalibrated, the prompt line dropped).
- Edges from ELK's orthogonal sections (`edgeCoords: ROOT`), drawn as polylines with 4px corners; layer gap 96, node gap 24, network-simplex placement, model order kept for stability. Requires is solid with an arrowhead, a condition gate dotted with a hollow diamond, a stage opening dotted with a bar, a dates-only link dotted at half strength with no marker; steel while waiting, border grey once satisfied; a stand-in edge takes the strongest style and shows its count. No text on lines: hovering an edge thickens it, outlines both ends, and shows a sentence ("Applies only if Who runs testing? = Partner; waiting"). Clicking an edge selects it and opens the edge card (8.7); edges take focus with Tab.
- Selecting a node traces it: upstream in ink, downstream in the accent, finished upstream lines faint, a Needs or Unblocks tag on each traced card, everything else faded to the one fade level. A one-line trace bar ("Who runs testing? - needs 1, unblocks 7 - 3 outside this view - Reveal - Esc"); Reveal expands the containers holding traced nodes and shows traced not-relevant nodes ghosted. The trace toggle and its address parameter go.
- Input at 720px and wider: two-finger scroll pans freely, the wheel alone never zooms, a pinch or Ctrl/Cmd+wheel zooms at the pointer, double-click expands. A small `gesture*` listener zooms on a Safari pinch only when no ctrl-wheel arrived since the gesture began (may follow in its own change; a real-device check is left to the user and noted in the proof). The attribution stays hidden; xyflow is credited on You > Licenses (8.4) and in the README's "Built with" list, and the README's deployment notes pass on xyflow's request that organizations hiding it commercially subscribe to Pro or sponsor. The controls restyled as square hairline buttons.
- The Select mode (from the journey menu, Edit structure, and route drafts): shift-click and shift-drag select, a band says so, Esc leaves; the selection bar with the bulk actions, shared with 8.10. Outside it, shift does nothing special.

In, ladder and lens:
- The detail ladder (Stages, Decisions, Work, All; keys 1 to 4; Stages left out with no top-level groups) replacing the kind checkboxes, over 8.3's level request. Two rules decide what is open: the step (at Stages, current stages open to Work and are labeled current) and the viewer's expand and collapse clicks, kept in the address until a step is picked. Expand with the container's plus control, double-click, or Enter; an expand refits to it; `f` fits the selection.
- Zoom sets only what a card draws, through three bands on the canvas root (CSS only, no re-render): near draws the full card; mid draws the head and title; far draws the title led by a glyph whose shape names the state, and no corner ticks. The minimum zoom is computed from the laid-out bounds, so fit-all always fits.
- View menu: Signals (off, rank, gravity, unblocks, slack; one labeled chip in each card's bottom-right slot, tinted on the ramp by quartile, with a removable chip showing which lens is on; containers show peak gravity naming the node), Show origins, and Key (the edge legend). The hidden count ("4 not relevant hidden - Show") bottom-left. Filters and the decisions toggle fade; with a selection the trace decides what is full strength; fades never multiply.
- Stability: selection kept by key, expand state in the address, and the viewport across refetches; ELK takes previous positions as hints; auto-fit only on first open, a step, and expand or collapse.

Out: Edit structure's authoring tools (8.11); the timeline (8.10); keyboard following of edges by arrow keys (later).
