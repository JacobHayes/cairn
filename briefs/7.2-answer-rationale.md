# 7.2: A rationale on answers

| | |
|---|---|

| Crates or packages | `crates/schema`, `crates/engine`, `crates/store`, `crates/store-turso`, `crates/api`, `crates/mcp`, `openapi/`, `web/client`, `web/app` |

## Goal

Let an answer say why it was given. A decision's answer, and each revision of it, may carry a markdown rationale, shown with the answer wherever the answer is shown and kept with every earlier answer in its history, so an answer can itself be the record of what was decided and why, with no separate deliverable to hold the reasoning.

## Governing

- PRD: B2 (rationale on answer and revise; reopen clears it), C8 (node detail, rendered as markdown), C11 (the answer form on a card), C12 (the decision list), Core concepts (Answer), J1 (answer set/changed carries it), J3 (replay).
- ARCHITECTURE: Schema outline (`answers`), Generated artifacts.
- PRACTICES: Explicit limits (a rationale is free text under the existing free-text limit).

## Scope

In: the rationale field on the answer and revise mutations and on stored answers; replay; the store schema and both backends; the API, OpenAPI, the TypeScript client, and the MCP answer tool. On screen, with 8.7 and 8.9: the WHY field of the inspector's answer form, which the Next page's cards share, open and optional, one line that grows; saved, it renders as markdown with the app's existing markdown component, with Edit reason on a decided decision (editing only the reason is a revision with the current text). Choosing a different answer empties WHY, with "Previous reason" and Reuse to copy it in on purpose, so nothing carries forward silently. Short forms are plain text: a mark on a decided decision's graph card whose hover shows the first paragraph with markdown stripped (8.8), and the Why column of the Plan list with decisions on (8.10). History shows each answer with its own reason or "no reason given". Reopen's menu label says it clears the answer and its reason. The model, API, and MCP parts do not wait for the screens.

Out: a rationale on any other transition (skip already takes a reason), and message-draft placeholders for a rationale.

## Acceptance

- Behavior by id: B2 (answer with a rationale; revise with a new one, with none, and back; reopen clears answer and rationale), C8, C12, J1, J3.
- Scenario over the hiring loop: answer a decision with a rationale, revise it without one, revise again with a different one; node detail shows only the current rationale, history shows all three answers each with its own rationale or none, and replay matches stored state field by field.
- Playwright: answer on a Next page card and in the inspector with a rationale holding a list and a link; it renders as markdown in the inspector and History and as plain text in the graph card's hover and the Plan list's Why column; choosing a different answer empties WHY and offers Reuse.
- Planted bug: carry the previous rationale forward on a revision that gives none, and show the scenario fail.

## Decisions left to the implementer

- Where the plain-text short form is cut (about 200 characters on hover, one line in the column).

## Decision log

- The rationale is a separate `rationales` map on the journey state beside `answers`, not a
  change to the answer's own type, so every stored document and event keeps parsing. On the
  wire the answer mutation and the answer record each gain an optional `rationale`; in the
  store it is a nullable column on the answer's row, so a revision that gives none stores
  null and removing an answer removes both.
- The rationale is `Markdown` (the existing free-text limit). An empty or blank rationale is
  refused rather than stored, so "none" has one spelling; the answer form leaves the field
  off when it is blank.
- A graph holding a rationale beside no answer is invalid (a state-stage violation).
- Changes the system makes to an answer's value (mapping a removed choice onto the route's
  choices on upgrade, moving a date answer to resolve a shortfall) keep that answer's
  rationale, since they revise the value and not the reason.
- The decision view's entry and the snapshot carry the rationale only while the answer is in
  effect, as they do the answer; node detail returns it as recorded.
- The answer form shows the field open. Its draft is kept apart from the answer's draft, so
  drafts saved before this change still load.
