# 7.2: A rationale on answers

## Goal

Let an answer say why it was given. A decision's answer, and each revision of it, may carry a markdown rationale, shown with the answer wherever the answer is shown and kept with every earlier answer in its history, so an answer can itself be the record of what was decided and why, with no separate deliverable to hold the reasoning.

## Governing

- PRD: B2 (rationale on answer and revise; reopen clears it), C8 (node detail), C11 (the answer editor on a card), C12 (decision view), Core concepts (Answer), J1 (answer set/changed carries it), J3 (replay).
- ARCHITECTURE: Schema outline (`answers`), Generated artifacts.
- PRACTICES: Explicit limits (a rationale is free text under the existing free-text limit).

## Scope

In: the rationale field on the answer and revise mutations and on stored answers; replay; the store schema and both backends; the API, OpenAPI, the TypeScript client, and the MCP answer tool; the answer editor in node detail and on triage cards; the rationale in node detail, in the decision history, and in the decision view.

Out: a rationale on any other transition (skip already takes a reason), and message-draft placeholders for a rationale.

## Acceptance

- Behavior by id: B2 (answer with a rationale; revise with a new one, with none, and back; reopen clears answer and rationale), C8, C12, J1, J3.
- Scenario over the hiring loop: answer a decision with a rationale, revise it without one, revise again with a different one; node detail shows only the current rationale, history shows all three answers each with its own rationale or none, and replay matches stored state field by field.
- Playwright: answer from a triage card with a rationale and see it in node detail and the decision view.
- Planted bug: carry the previous rationale forward on a revision that gives none, and show the scenario fail.

## Decisions left to the implementer

- Whether the answer editor shows the rationale field open or behind a "why" toggle.
