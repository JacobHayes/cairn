## Goal

Make a reusable piece of a process a first-class object. A segment is a closed graph, versioned and drafted like a route, that is inserted into a route draft or a journey rather than started as a journey. Inserting one copies its nodes with fresh keys, maps its roles and kinds onto the graph it joins, and wires it in with edges in the same patch. Each insertion keeps its lineage, so a newer segment version can be merged into it the way a route upgrade merges into a journey, and part of any route or journey can be saved as a new segment. Many processes share recurring pieces while differing in their overall shape, so this lets a team keep those pieces in one place instead of copying them between routes or rebuilding them by hand in each journey.

## Scope

In:

- The segment domain: create, draft, publish, retire, import and export, and its revision, beside routes and sharing their machinery wherever the behaviour is the same.
- The closed-graph checks and the segment-only invariants.
- The insert mutation, for route drafts and journeys: key minting from the patch id, role and kind mapping (including the two-filling-decisions rule), and edges to the host in the same patch.
- Insertion records, and the `from_segment` provenance.
- Insertion upgrade as a B7-style merge proposal, keyed by segment key within the insertion.
- Save as segment, with B8's review and boundary edges dropped and listed; once the draft is published, the re-link proposal that makes the source nodes an insertion of the version that published it.
- The API, OpenAPI, the TypeScript client, MCP tools, and the shipped agent instructions (how and when to insert a segment).
- A new fixture: a generic segment inserted twice into one journey of an existing fixture route.

Out: the screens (7.7), live or by-reference inclusion (`decisions/2026-10-09-a-segment-is-copied-in-with-lineage-not-included-live.md`), and nesting a segment inside a segment.

## Acceptance

- Behavior by id: A21 (a draft whose condition reaches outside it, or that declares `final`, is rejected with paths), B13 (two insertions of one version into one journey get distinct keys; a role mapped to an existing role resolves participations through it; a mapping giving a role two filling decisions is rejected; edges to the host land in the same patch), B14 (local edits kept, a changed field merged, a removed node orphaned with keep or remove, conflicts surfaced; a route upgrade leaves `from_segment` nodes alone), B15 (a crossing edge is dropped and listed; after publishing, the re-link proposal makes the source an insertion of that version), A13 (segment file round trip), H5 (segment revision conflicts), J3 (the PRD's new scenarios replay field by field).
- The fixture and its scenario in `fixtures/` with the derived values its README records, checked like the others.
- Planted bug: reuse the segment's keys on insertion, and show the second insertion rejected by the key invariant.

- The insert mutation's wire shape and how an agent names role and kind mappings.
